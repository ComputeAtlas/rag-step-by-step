## Which location should I use?

| I want to | Go to |
|---|---|
| Buy a bike and ride it first | Any store or Sales-level dealer |
| Have a bike serviced | A Fernhill service centre or a Service-level dealer |
| Replace a motor, controller or display, or get a warranty repair | A Master Service location |
| Test ride a Cargo 700 | A location marked "Cargo 700 test rides" |
| Recycle a battery | Any Fernhill dealer or store |
| Collect a part you ordered | Delivery to a dealer is free (see shipping and delivery) |

## Collection from home

Within 30 miles of a Fernhill service centre we can collect your bike and bring it back for £39 each way (see service and
repair pricing).

## Getting to the Hollinwick Flagship

The Hollinwick Flagship is at 14 Mill Lane, five minutes' walk from Hollinwick railway station. There are 20 free parking spaces behind the
building, including 4 spaces with electric vehicle chargers and 2 spaces for vans, which are large enough for a Cargo 700 on a trailer. Bicycle
racks are at the front. The store has step-free access, and a viewing window shows the assembly line to visitors.

The Flagship holds one of every model on the shop floor, plus test bikes you can ride on the test track behind the factory. Test rides are free
and take about 20 minutes; bring a form of ID. Under-16s must be accompanied by an adult.
