# About Fernhill Bikes

Fernhill Bikes Ltd designs, builds and services electric bikes. We make three models: the Ridge 500 for the trail, the
Metro 300 for the city and the Cargo 700 for carrying people and loads. We were founded in 2017 and our headquarters,
factory and flagship store are in the town of Hollinwick, on the edge of the Calder Moor valley in the north of England.

## Our story

Two engineers started Fernhill in 2017 in a rented barn at Hollinwick. They had spent years fixing other people's
electric bikes and noticed that the same things went wrong again and again: sealed batteries nobody could replace,
parts that could not be bought and manuals that said very little. They decided to build a bike that could be repaired
for ten years instead of thrown away after three.

Ireland and the EU.

## How we work

- **Repair first.** Every part we sell has a published part number and price, and we keep parts for at least seven
  years after the last bike of a model is sold.
- **Plain manuals.** Owners get a manual with real tables: torque values, tyre pressures, error codes.
- **Honest ranges.** We publish how our range figures were measured (flat road, 75 kg rider, 20 °C), and how much cold weather and
  hills reduce them.
- **Batteries get recycled.** Any Fernhill dealer takes back a battery for free. Our recycling partner recovers about 90% of the
  weight of each pack.

## Quality and testing

- Every bike is ride-tested on the 2 km test track behind the Hollinwick factory before it is boxed.
- New battery designs are cycled in our lab for 300 full charge cycles before they go on sale. We measure the capacity every
  25 cycles.
- One frame in every 50 is tested to destruction on a rig, and all frame welds are inspected by eye.
- Each charger is tested at full load for 4 hours. After the 2025 charger safety notice (SN-2025-03), the test time was extended
  from 1 hour to 4 hours.
- Firmware is tested on every combination of model and battery type before release. Release notes list known issues honestly,
  including the ones we have not fixed yet.

## How the range is measured

Our claimed range figures come from tests on a flat tarmac loop with a 75 kg rider, at 20 °C, with the tyres at the pressure in
the manual. Each assist level is tested separately. The Cargo 700 is also tested fully loaded at 200 kg. We publish the figures per
assist level in each owner's manual, because a single "up to" number hides how much the assist level matters.

## Frequently asked about Fernhill

**Where are Fernhill bikes made?** In Hollinwick. We assemble the frames, motors and batteries into bikes there. Components are bought in from
suppliers in Europe and Asia.

**Do you sell bikes through shops?** Yes. We sell directly at fernhillbikes.example, in our five stores and through the dealer network.

**Do you offer finance to individuals?** The only payment plan we offer is the business plan for Cargo 700 fleets. See the business plan
document.

**Can I visit the factory?** Factory visits are not offered. The Hollinwick Flagship, in the same town, has a viewing window onto the assembly line.
