## Customer's question

Zane wrote: "My Ridge 500 brake pads are down to the metal after only 700 km. I want them replaced under warranty."

## Conversation

**Rhys:** I understand the frustration, Zane. Brake pads are wear parts, and the warranty does not cover parts that wear out with normal riding, so
I can't treat this as a warranty claim. Do you ride in muddy or gritty conditions?

**Zane:** Almost every weekend, in the hills.

**Rhys:** That wears organic pads quickly, especially if the brakes are not checked. The manual says to check the pads every 500 km and replace them at
1.5 mm. At 700 km you would have passed the check. The Ridge 500 pads are FH-3105-A at £24 per wheel set.

**Zane:** Can you do anything?

**Rhys:** I can send a Ridge 500 service kit as a goodwill gesture. FH-9001-A contains a chain, two brake pad sets and chain lube, normally £89. Please
also check the rotors for scoring; a rotor below 1.8 mm should be replaced (FH-3205-A, £32).

**Zane:** That is generous, thank you.

## Resolution

Not a warranty matter, because pads are a wear part. As a goodwill gesture one Ridge 500 service kit (FH-9001-A) was sent.
Customer reminded of the 500 km pad check.
