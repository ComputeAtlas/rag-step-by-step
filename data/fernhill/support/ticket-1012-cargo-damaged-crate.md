## Customer's question

Idris wrote the evening his Cargo 700 arrived: "The crate has a big hole in one side and the display is cracked. The driver
left before I opened it."

## Conversation

**Callum:** I am sorry, Idris. Thank you for writing straight away. Under our delivery terms you have 48 hours to report
damage, so you are well within it. Could you send photos of the crate, the cracked display and the delivery note?

**Idris:** Photos attached. The delivery note has no comment on damage because the driver was in a hurry.

**Callum:** That is fine. The photos show impact damage to the crate and a crack across the display screen. Does the rest of the bike look right: frame, wheels, rack,
kickstand?

**Idris:** All fine as far as I can see.

**Callum:** Then we only need to replace the display. The Cargo 700 uses the Dash 2, part FH-5102-A, which normally costs £189. We
will send one free of charge, with the instructions for fitting it. Because the display and the controller must carry the
same firmware version, update the bike afterwards with the Fernhill Service app, or the display may show E14.

**Idris:** Do I have to send back the damaged one?

**Callum:** Please keep it until the new one is fitted, and then return it with the prepaid label.

*27 August 2025.* Idris confirmed the new display works; the bike was ridden 20 km without errors.

## Resolution

Transit damage reported within 48 hours. Display FH-5102-A replaced free. Customer reminded that display and controller
firmware must match (E14).

## Note for the support team

Damage must be reported within 48 hours of delivery. After a display replacement, check the firmware versions match.
