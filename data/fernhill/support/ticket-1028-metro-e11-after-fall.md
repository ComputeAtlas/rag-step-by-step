## Customer's question

Tamsin wrote: "My Metro 300 fell over when parked, and now it shows E11 every time I switch it on. The motor does not work."

## Conversation

**Rhys:** On the Metro 300, E11 means the brake cut-off switch is active at switch-on. When a brake lever is pressed, the
bike cuts the motor for safety. After a fall the lever may be bent or stuck. Does either lever stay pulled, or feel stiff?

**Tamsin:** The left one looks bent in a little and doesn't spring back fully.

**Rhys:** That is almost certainly it. First, try freeing the lever by hand and checking it springs back, then restart. If it still
shows E11, the lever or its switch is damaged. The Metro 300 brake lever pair with cut-off switch is FH-3311-A at £31.

**Tamsin:** I straightened it a bit but it is still stuck.

**Rhys:** Then please book a dealer to fit the new lever pair. Damage from a fall is not covered by the warranty, so the part
and fitting are charged. Fitting is a short job at £48 per hour.

*10 March 2026.* The dealer fitted FH-3311-A. E11 gone; the bike works.

## Resolution

Bent brake lever from a fall kept the cut-off switch active (E11). Lever pair FH-3311-A fitted by a dealer at the
customer's cost. Crash damage is excluded from the warranty.
