**Isla:** The photos show a dented box and damage that fits an impact in transit rather than a fall. I have asked my
team and we will make an exception. We'll send a new mudguard set and rear light free of charge. The Metro 300 rear light is FH-8110-A (£22) and the mudguard

*12 June 2026.* Parts arrived and were fitted.

Late report (day 4, limit 48 hours) accepted as a goodwill gesture because the photos showed transit damage. Rear light FH-8110-A and mudguard set FH-8301-A sent free.
