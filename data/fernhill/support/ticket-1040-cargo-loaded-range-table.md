## Customer's question

Imani phoned: "My Cargo 700 delivers about 45 km on a charge when I carry 100 kg of parcels. The website said 100 km."

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Which assist level are you in?

**Imani:** Tour mostly. Sport on hills.

**Hannah:** The 100 km figure is for Eco, unloaded. Your manual has a table for loaded riding: fully loaded at 200 kg total, Tour gives
up to 55 km and Sport up to 40 km. With 100 kg of parcels plus yourself you are somewhere between the two cases, so 45 km in Tour and Sport mixed is
normal.

**Imani:** Is there anything that helps?

**Hannah:** Set the Load menu to Half or Full so the range estimate is honest. Keep the tyres at 3.2 bar when loaded. Use Eco on
flat stretches. If the round is long, charge during the day. The Cargo 700 charges from 0 to 80% in 3 hours.

**Imani:** Fine. Is the battery healthy?

**Hannah:** The battery health test is quick and free with a service. If the display shows over 90% health, there is no battery fault.

## Resolution

No fault. Range is consistent with the loaded range table for the Cargo 700. Advice on the Load menu, assist levels and tyre pressure.

## Note for the support team

Use the loaded range table in the Cargo 700 manual. Set the Load menu so the estimate is realistic.
