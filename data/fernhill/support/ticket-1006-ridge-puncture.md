## Customer's question

Fenn wrote: "I have had three punctures on my Ridge 500 in a month, all in the rear tube. Is there a problem with the
tyres? Are they covered?"

## Conversation

**Callum:** Sorry to hear that, Fenn. Tyres and inner tubes are wear parts, so they are not covered by the warranty, but
three rear punctures in a month usually have a cause. What pressure do you run?

**Fenn:** About 1.0 bar for the trails because I thought low is better.

**Callum:** For off-road the manual says 1.4 to 2.0 bar. At 1.0 bar the tyre can pinch the tube against the rim edge on a
rock, called a pinch flat, which looks like two small holes side by side. Is that what you see?

**Fenn:** Yes, two little holes.

**Callum:** That fits. Raise the pressure to 1.5 or 1.6 bar to start. The rear tyre carries more weight and takes the
harder hits. If you want to replace the tube, the Ridge 500 tube is FH-4190-A (£8), and the rear tyre is FH-4101-B (£42).
The front tyre is FH-4101-A; do not order that one for the rear. Check the inside of the tyre with your fingers for thorns
before refitting.

**Fenn:** I will do that. Thanks.

## Resolution

Pinch flats from running 1.0 bar. Advised 1.4 to 2.0 bar off-road. Customer to buy a new tube (FH-4190-A) if needed.
No warranty claim as tubes and tyres are wear parts.

## Note for the support team

Tyres and tubes are wear parts and not covered. FH-4101-A is the front tyre and FH-4101-B the rear.
