## Customer's question

Quentin phoned: "My Metro 300 shows E04 and the motor does not help at all. I bought it in March this year. I looked for a
magnet on the wheel in the manual, but I cannot find one."

## Conversation

**Isla:** The reason you cannot find a magnet is that the Metro 300 does not use one. On this model E04 means the hub motor's
speed signal is missing, and the speed sensor is inside the motor. On the Ridge 500 and Cargo 700, E04 is a separate speed
sensor with a magnet, but the Metro 300 is different.

**Quentin:** So I cannot fix it myself?

**Isla:** That is what the manual says: "Cannot be fixed at home: see a dealer." First, switch the bike off for ten seconds
and check that the motor cable plug near the rear dropout is pushed home. That is E02, but a loose plug can also cause this.

**Quentin:** I checked the plug. It is tight. The code stays.

**Isla:** Then please book a Master Service dealer. Your bike was bought in March 2025, so the 2025 warranty policy
applies. The motor and controller are covered for 2 years, so the repair is free. Write to warranty@fernhillbikes.example
with your serial number and the order number, and send a photo of the display.

*Follow-up, 18 July 2025.* The dealer confirmed a fault in the motor's speed sensor and fitted a new hub motor wheel
assembly (FH-6108-A) under warranty. The ticket is closed.

## Resolution

Fault in the Metro 300 hub motor (E04 means the built-in speed sensor lost its signal). Motor replaced free of charge
under the 2025 warranty policy, which covers motor and controller for 2 years. The replaced motor is covered for the rest
of the original warranty period.

## Note for the support team

Metro 300 E04 is the hub motor's built-in speed sensor, not a wheel magnet. 2025 policy: motor covered for 2 years.
