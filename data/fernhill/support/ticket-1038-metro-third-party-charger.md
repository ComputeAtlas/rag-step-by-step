## Customer's question

Olu wrote: "My Metro 300 battery will not hold a charge after two months. I bought the bike on 12 March 2026. Please replace it under
warranty."

## Conversation

**Callum:** I'm sorry, Olu. The battery warranty for a bike bought in 2026 is 3 years or 800 cycles, so on time alone you would be covered. Before
I send the claim on, I need to ask: which charger do you use?

**Olu:** I lost the one from the box in the move and bought a cheaper one online. It fits the port.

**Callum:** That matters. The warranty policy says batteries charged with any charger other than the Fernhill charger supplied
with the bike are not covered. A charger from another maker can charge at the wrong voltage and damage the cells.

**Olu:** The charger says 36 V.

**Callum:** The voltage may match, but we cannot guarantee how it controls the current and the cut-off. A dealer's health test showed 31%
capacity, which matches charging damage. I'm sorry, I can't approve this under the warranty.

**Olu:** What are my options?

**Callum:** A new Metro 300 battery is FH-2219-B at £329. For the future, the right charger is FH-2310-B, £54, the same part number as the
one supplied. A genuine replacement bought from Fernhill is treated as identical to the supplied one, so it does not affect your warranty.

**Olu:** I'll buy the battery and a genuine charger.

*1 June 2026.* Both parts were ordered and delivered. Total £398 (£329 for the battery, £54 for the charger and the £15 battery delivery fee).

## Resolution

Warranty claim declined: the battery was charged with a non-Fernhill charger, which the 2026 policy excludes. Customer
bought FH-2219-B (£329) and FH-2310-B (£54), plus £15 battery delivery.

## Note for the support team

Non-Fernhill charger: battery not covered. A genuine replacement bought from Fernhill is fine. Battery delivery costs £15.
