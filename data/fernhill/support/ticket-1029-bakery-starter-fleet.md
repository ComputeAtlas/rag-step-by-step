## Customer's question

Oskar phoned: "I run a bakery with a delivery round and want to put four Cargo 700 bikes on it. Is delivery use covered? What does it cost
per month? And what do I pay if a battery wears out?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Four bikes puts you in the Starter tier of the business plan, which is for 3 to 9 bikes: £139 per bike per month over 36 months. That is £556 per month for
four bikes. Bikes bought on a Fernhill business plan are covered for delivery and rental use. On the standard warranty a delivery
bike would not be.

**Oskar:** What does the warranty give me?

**Hannah:** The warranty for the year of purchase. For bikes bought in 2026 it is 2 years on the motor and controller, and 3 years or 800 cycles on the battery. If you register each bike within 30 days, the battery warranty
grows to 4 years, with the same 800-cycle limit.

**Oskar:** And after the battery has done its 800 cycles?

**Hannah:** The Cargo 700 battery is FH-2292-A. In the parts catalogue it costs £599. On the business plan you pay £499
for a new one.

**Oskar:** And at the end of the 36 months?

**Hannah:** You can keep a bike for £1,150, return it, or renew. I will send you the agreement. Delivery to one address takes 6 to 8
weeks.

**Oskar:** Please send it.

## Resolution

Customer advised on the business plan Starter tier (4 bikes at £139 each per month, £556 in total). Delivery use is
covered on a plan. Plan customers pay £499 for a replacement FH-2292-A battery instead of £599. Agreement sent by email.
