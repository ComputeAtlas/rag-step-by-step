## Customer's question

Kavya wrote: "I saw your safety notice about chargers. My Ridge 500 came in May. How do I know whether mine is affected?
It does not feel hot."

## Conversation

**Isla:** Thanks for checking, Kavya. Turn the charger over and look at the label. Two things matter: the part number and
the date code printed beside the serial number. The notice covers part number FH-2310-A with a date code of 0325, 0425,
0525 or 0625. Chargers supplied after 30 June 2025 are the revised FH-2310-B and are not affected. It does not matter if
yours feels cool; the problem shows up during several hours of charging.

**Kavya:** It says FH-2310-A, date code 0525.

**Isla:** Then it is covered. I have created a free replacement. We will send an FH-2310-B within five working days with a
prepaid label for the old one. Please do not leave the old charger plugged in unattended while you wait.

**Kavya:** I ride a lot in the evening. Can I charge for an hour or two when I am in the room?

**Isla:** Yes, short supervised charging is fine until the new one arrives. Never overnight.

*Follow-up, 12 August 2025.* Replacement received. Old charger returned.

## Resolution

Charger FH-2310-A, date code 0525: replaced free under safety notice SN-2025-03 with FH-2310-B. The notice applies only to
FH-2310-A chargers with date codes 0325 to 0625.

## Note for the support team

Chargers made after 30 June 2025 are FH-2310-B and are not affected. Cargo 700 chargers are never affected.
