## Customer's question

Carys wrote: "I noticed a crack in the weld where the down tube meets the head tube on my Ridge 500. I bought it on 14 March 2025 and it has
never crashed. Is the frame covered?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Please stop riding the bike, Carys. A crack at that joint is a safety issue. You bought the bike on 14 March 2025, so the
2025 warranty policy applies. Under it the frame is covered for 5 years for the original owner, so you are well inside the period.
(Bikes bought from 2026 have a lifetime frame warranty for the original owner, but that is a different policy.)

**Carys:** I am the original owner. What do you need?

**Hannah:** Write to warranty@fernhillbikes.example with your order number, the serial number from under the frame (it starts
with `FH-R5-25-`) and close photos of the crack. Under the 2025 policy we reply within 5 working days and repair or replace within 20 working days.

*Follow-up.* The photos showed a weld fault, not impact damage. The warranty team approved a new frame within 3 working days. A Master
Service dealer moved the motor, battery, display and wheels onto the new frame at no cost to Carys. The new frame is
covered for the rest of the original 5 years.

## Resolution

Weld fault. Frame replaced under the 2025 policy (frame cover 5 years, original owner). Parts moved to the new frame free of
charge. Customer stopped riding when the crack was found.

## Note for the support team

2025 policy frame cover is 5 years; the 2026 policy gives a lifetime frame warranty to bikes bought from 2026.
