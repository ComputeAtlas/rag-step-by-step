## Customer's question

Bertil asked in chat: "I bought a Metro 300 on 3 February 2026 and registered it on 20 February. How long is the battery warranty,
and is there a limit on charge cycles?"

## Conversation

**Callum:** Congratulations on the bike, Bertil. You bought it after 1 January 2026, so the 2026 warranty policy applies. The battery
is covered for 3 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. Because you registered within 30 days (17 days after
purchase), the battery warranty grows to 4 years. The 800-cycle limit stays the same.

**Bertil:** What counts as a defective battery?

**Callum:** One that holds less than 70% of its original capacity while it is still under warranty. The display shows battery health
under Menu > Battery.

**Bertil:** And how do I count cycles? Two charges a day?

**Callum:** No. One cycle is a total amount of energy equal to the battery's full capacity. Two top-ups of 50% make one cycle. The
count is shown under Menu > Battery > Cycles.

**Bertil:** Great.

## Resolution

Customer confirmed on the 2026 policy: battery 4 years (registered within 30 days) or 800 cycles, defective below 70% of
capacity. Cycle counting explained.

## Note for the support team

Registered within 30 days: 4-year battery warranty under the 2026 policy.
