## Customer's question

Amara wrote: "I bought my Ridge 500 three weeks ago. The website says up to 110 km but I get about 55 km on a full charge.
Is my battery faulty? I ride 12 km to work each way and the temperature is around 1 or 2 degrees."

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Thanks for getting in touch, Amara. The 110 km figure is the best case: Eco level, flat road, a 75 kg rider and
20 °C. Could you tell me which assist level you use, your tyre pressure and whether you keep the bike indoors overnight?

**Amara:** I use Boost most of the time because there is a long hill on the way home. The tyres are what they were when I
unpacked it. The bike stays in an unheated garage.

**Hannah:** That explains most of it. Boost gives the motor 220% of your effort and the manual's range table puts it at up to 45 km
on flat ground, so 55 km with a hill in the mix is actually good. The cold garage matters too: a cold battery gives
about a quarter less range near 0 °C. Two things to try: bring the battery indoors the night before you ride, and drop
to Tour (up to 80 km) on the flat parts. For the tyres, the Ridge 500 should be at 2.2 to 2.5 bar on the road.

**Amara:** I checked and they were at 1.2 bar. I have pumped them up.

**Hannah:** That alone will give you noticeably more distance. If you still see less than about 70 km in Tour on mild days, write
back and we will arrange a battery health test.

**Amara:** Thank you. I will try that.

## Resolution

No fault. The shortfall came from Boost level, a cold battery and soft tyres. Advice given on assist levels, indoor
storage of the battery in winter, and road tyre pressure of 2.2 to 2.5 bar. Customer to report back if the range stays low.

## Note for the support team

Cold weather and soft tyres are the two most common reasons for 'half range' tickets; check the winter riding guide before escalating.
