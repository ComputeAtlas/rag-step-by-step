## Customer's question

Noor wrote: "After updating my Cargo 700 to v2.6 the display shows that the next service is due at 2,000 km. The manual says
every 1,500 km. Which is right?"

## Conversation

**Callum:** The manual is right, Noor. The Cargo 700 needs a full service every 1,500 km or 6 months, whichever comes first,
because it works harder than the other models. Firmware v2.6 added a service reminder, and the release notes list a known
issue: on the Cargo 700 it counts 2,000 km instead of 1,500 km. A fix is planned for v2.7, which is not yet released.

**Noor:** So I should ignore the screen?

**Callum:** Please follow the manual for the distance, and the reminder for the time interval. Book the service at 1,500 km or 6
months from the last one, whichever comes first. A Cargo 700 full service costs £139.

**Noor:** Done. I'll book it at 1,400 km.

## Resolution

Known issue in firmware v2.6: Cargo 700 service reminder uses 2,000 km. Correct interval is 1,500 km or 6 months. Fix
planned for v2.7 (not yet released).

## Note for the support team

v2.6 Cargo 700 service reminder counts 2,000 km. Real interval 1,500 km or 6 months. Fix expected in v2.7.
