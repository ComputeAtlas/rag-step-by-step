## Customer's question

Wen wrote: "I ordered a set of FH-3105-A brake pads for my Cargo 700 because they said 4-piston on the page. They arrived and
my dealer says they are the wrong ones. Can I swap them?"

## Conversation

**Rhys:** Hi Wen. Your dealer is right. FH-3105-A is the organic pad for the Ridge 500. The Cargo 700 needs FH-3105-B,
which is the sintered heavy-duty compound. The two fit the same caliper shape, but the organic pad overheats under the
loads a Cargo 700 carries. The catalogue lists both numbers, and the final letter marks the variant, not a newer version.

**Wen:** Sorry, I did not realise the letter mattered.

**Rhys:** It is an easy mistake. As the parts arrived less than 14 days ago and are unused in their packaging, you can
return them. Please send the FH-3105-A set back; we will refund £24 and send FH-3105-B for £29. I will waive the return postage
because our page was not clear enough.

**Wen:** Thank you. Which pad do I need for both wheels?

**Rhys:** The same FH-3105-B for front and rear, but you need one set per wheel. If you need both, order two. The
catalogue price is £29 per set.

**Wen:** I will order two sets.

*10 June 2025.* Wen confirmed the correct pads were fitted and the brakes feel strong.

## Resolution

Wrong part ordered (FH-3105-A, Ridge 500, instead of FH-3105-B, Cargo 700). Unused FH-3105-A returned within the 14-day
window and refunded; customer bought two sets of FH-3105-B at £29 each. Return postage waived. Pads are a wear part and
would not have been covered by the warranty.

## Note for the support team

The final letter of a brake pad number marks a variant, not a revision. The parts catalogue lists common ordering mistakes.
