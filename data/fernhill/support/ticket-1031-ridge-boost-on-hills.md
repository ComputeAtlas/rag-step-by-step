## Customer's question

Hugo asked: "On a hilly 40 km ride my Ridge 500 battery is nearly empty. The website says 110 km. I always use Boost on the hills
and Sport on the flat."

## Conversation

**Rhys:** That combination explains it, Hugo. The Ridge 500 manual lists the ranges: Eco 110 km, Tour 80 km, Sport 60 km and Boost 45 km, all on the flat with a 75 kg rider at
20 °C. Boost is the shortest range because the motor gives 220% of your effort. Mixed with Sport on the flat you can
expect about 40 to 50 km.

**Hugo:** Is Sport on the flat a waste?

**Rhys:** On the flat you rarely need more than Tour. Try Tour on level ground and Sport on climbs, and keep Boost for short steep
bursts. Also shift down before a climb, so the motor and your legs share the work. The motor stays cooler too, which avoids E08.

**Hugo:** I'll test that this weekend.

**Rhys:** Another option is Battery care mode if you rarely need the full range: it stops charging at 80%. For long rides, switch
it off and charge to 100%.

## Resolution

No fault. Range consistent with Boost and Sport use on hills. Advice on assist level, gear choice and Battery care mode.

## Note for the support team

Use the range table in the Ridge 500 manual. Boost gives the shortest range.
