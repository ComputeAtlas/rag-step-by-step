## Customer's question

Esme wrote: "I run a small flower delivery round and bought a Cargo 700 on 6 November 2025. After three weeks the display shows
E19 and the motor will not turn. Please can you repair it under warranty?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** I am sorry about the fault, Esme. E19 is a motor phase fault, and the manual says not to ride the bike and to see a dealer.
I need to check the warranty first. You bought the bike on 6 November 2025, so the 2025 warranty policy applies. Under that
policy the motor is covered for 2 years, but any bike used for rental or delivery work is excluded, with no exceptions. The
Fernhill business plan, which does allow Cargo 700 delivery use, only starts on 1 January 2026 and is for fleets of three or more.

**Esme:** I did not know that. The website did not say.

**Hannah:** It is in the policy under "What is not covered". I have asked my colleagues whether we can make an exception, and
they say no. I am sorry. What we can do is repair it at the price list. The motor is FH-6110-A. The exchange price is £399
when you return the old motor within 14 days. Fitting a Cargo 700 motor is 2 hours of labour at £48 per hour, which is £96.
The total is £495.

**Esme:** That is a lot, but I need the bike. Please go ahead.

**Hannah:** I will book it at the Master Service dealer closest to you. In the future, if you buy at least three bikes on a
business plan, delivery use is covered. For one bike, the plan does not apply.

*2 December 2025.* The motor was replaced and the bike is back on the road.

## Resolution

Not a warranty repair. The bike was bought in 2025 and used for deliveries, which the 2025 policy excludes without
exceptions. Customer paid £495 (FH-6110-A exchange price £399 plus 2 hours of labour at £48 per hour).

## Note for the support team

Under the 2025 policy there is no delivery-use exception. The business plan only exists from 1 January 2026.
