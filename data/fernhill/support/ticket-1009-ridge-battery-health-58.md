## Customer's question

Dev wrote: "I bought my Ridge 500 on 5 February 2025. The range has fallen to about 45 km in Tour and Menu > Battery now
says battery health 58%. The cycle count shows 126. Surely a battery should not do this?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** You are right to ask, Dev. A battery with only 126 cycles should still be close to full health. Your bike was
bought in 2025, so the 2025 warranty policy applies: battery cover is 2 years or 500 cycles, and a battery counts as
defective if it holds less than 60% of its original capacity while under warranty. Yours reads 58%, and you are well
inside both limits.

**Dev:** Do you need anything from me?

**Hannah:** Please send the order number, the serial number from under the frame, and a photo of the battery screen to
warranty@fernhillbikes.example. Also tell me the firmware version, because I want to rule out a calibration problem (error E17).

**Dev:** It shows v2.4.

**Hannah:** That is the current release, so firmware is not the cause. I will ask a Master Service dealer to run the battery health test, which
takes about 15 minutes, so we are not relying only on the number on the display.

*Follow-up, 30 July 2025.* The dealer's test confirmed 57% capacity. The warranty team approved the claim. The old
FH-2291-A pack was replaced with a new FH-2291-B. The replacement is covered for the rest of the original warranty period.

## Resolution

Faulty battery pack. Under the 2025 policy the pack counts as defective below 60% of original capacity, and this one
measured 57% at 126 cycles. Replaced free by a dealer with FH-2291-B. The customer did not pay for the part or the
labour.

## Note for the support team

2025 policy threshold is 60% of original capacity (70% in the 2026 policy). Always check the purchase date on the receipt first.
