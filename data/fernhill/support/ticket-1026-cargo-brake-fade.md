## Customer's question

Yara phoned: "Coming down a long hill with two children on the back, my Cargo 700 brakes got weak and I had to stop to let them cool.
It frightened me. It has done 520 km."

## Conversation

**Tom:** Thank you for telling us, Yara. Brake fade on a loaded bike is a real safety matter. The manual says to check the pads on the
Cargo 700 every 400 km and to replace them at 1.5 mm. At 520 km you are past that check. Have they been looked at?

**Yara:** No.

**Tom:** Please stop using the bike with the children until the pads are checked. The Cargo 700 uses FH-3105-B, the sintered
heavy-duty pad, at £29 per wheel set. A worn pad overheats more easily. A fully loaded bike also needs about 40% more
stopping distance, so it is worth braking early and in a low gear on descents.

**Yara:** Where can I get that done today?

**Tom:** At a Service-level dealer, or I can book you into the Brackenford Service Centre. A full service for the Cargo 700 is
£139. If you only need pads fitted, the labour is charged at £48 per hour.

*20 February 2026.* The dealer found 0.8 mm of pad on the front and 1.1 mm on the rear and fitted two FH-3105-B sets (£58).
Braking back to normal.

## Resolution

Worn pads on a heavily loaded bike. Two sets of FH-3105-B fitted at the customer's cost, as pads are a wear part. Customer
reminded of the 400 km pad check interval and of the extra stopping distance with a full load.

## Note for the support team

Cargo 700 pad check interval is 400 km. Pads are FH-3105-B. Do not allow the customer to carry children until the pads are replaced.
