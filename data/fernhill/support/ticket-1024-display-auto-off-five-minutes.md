## Customer's question

Marek wrote in chat: "My manual and your FAQ both say the display switches itself off after 10 minutes. Mine now does it after about
5. Is my display broken?"

## Conversation

**Rhys:** Your display is fine, Marek. Did you update the firmware recently?

**Marek:** Yes, to v2.6 last week.

**Rhys:** That explains it. Firmware v2.6, released on 12 January 2026, changed the auto-off delay from 10 minutes to 5
minutes. The owner's manual (Edition 4, December 2025) and the FAQ were written before that and still say 10 minutes. The
release notes for v2.6 give the current figure: 5 minutes.

**Marek:** Can I change it back?

**Rhys:** Not at the moment; the delay is fixed in the firmware. The bike stays awake while you are riding, so it only affects a
parked bike.

**Marek:** Fine, I just wanted to be sure it wasn't a fault.

## Resolution

Not a fault. Firmware v2.6 reduced display auto-off from 10 to 5 minutes. Manual and FAQ are out of date on this point.

## Note for the support team

Auto-off changed from 10 to 5 minutes in v2.6. The Edition 4 manuals and the FAQ (18 December 2025) are out of date on this point.
