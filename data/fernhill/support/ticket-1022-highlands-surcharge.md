## Customer's question

Jess wrote: "Your website says delivery is free, but I was charged £49 for my Ridge 500. I live on an island off the west coast
of Scotland. Is that a mistake?"

## Conversation

**Isla:** It is not a mistake, Jess, but the checkout should have shown it more clearly. Delivery of bikes is free to
mainland UK addresses. For the Scottish Highlands and islands there is a £49 surcharge, and it adds 2 to 3 working days
to the usual 3 to 5 working days for the Ridge 500.

**Jess:** Is there a way round it?

**Isla:** Yes. You can have the bike delivered for free to a participating Fernhill dealer and collect it there, if one is
near you. The dealer can also set the bike up, for £49. If the dealer is not on your island, the surcharge is likely to
come out the same, but you avoid carrying a boxed bike home.

**Jess:** The nearest dealer is on the mainland, a ferry away. I will keep the home delivery.

**Isla:** That is a sensible choice. The bike should arrive within 5 to 8 working days of dispatch.

## Resolution

The £49 surcharge for the Highlands and islands is correct and listed in the shipping and delivery terms. Customer chose to
keep home delivery. Checkout wording passed to the web team.
