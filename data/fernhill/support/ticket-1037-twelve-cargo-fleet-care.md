## Customer's question

Pia wrote: "We are a courier firm planning to lease 12 Cargo 700 bikes. What would it cost per month, what is Fleet Care, and what
happens if we end the contract early?"

## Conversation

**Tom:** Twelve bikes is the Fleet tier (10 to 49 bikes). The price is £129 per bike per month for 36 months, so £1,548
per month for twelve. The price includes VAT, the 48 V charger and two keys for each bike.

**Pia:** What does Fleet Care cost, and what does it include?

**Tom:** Fleet Care is an add-on at £12 per bike per month, £144 for twelve bikes. It includes the 6-monthly service at your depot (every 1,500 km
or 6 months), quarterly battery health tests and a loan bike within 3 working days if one is off the road for more than two days.

**Pia:** And early termination?

**Tom:** If you end the plan before 36 months, you pay 3 monthly payments per bike. For twelve bikes at £129 that is 12 x 3 x £129, which is £4,644, and we collect the bikes at our cost.

**Pia:** What about the warranty for delivery use?

**Tom:** Bikes on a business plan are covered for delivery and rental use, under the warranty policy for the year of purchase. Register each bike within
30 days to get the 4-year battery cover under the 2026 policy.

**Pia:** Thank you, that is clear.

## Resolution

Quote given: 12 bikes at £129 per month each (£1,548 per month), Fleet Care at £12 per bike per month (£144 per month for
twelve), early termination fee of 3 monthly payments per bike (£4,644 for 12 bikes at the Fleet tier price).

## Note for the support team

Fleet tier is 10 to 49 bikes at £129. Early termination fee is 3 monthly payments per bike.
