## Customer's question

Sven phoned: "I was going up a steep ramp with two crates of tools and the Cargo cut out and showed E15. I restarted it and it
worked again. Is something broken?"

## Conversation

**Callum:** No. On the Cargo 700, E15 means the controller over-current protection acted. It often happens when the bike stalls on
a slope with a heavy load, which is just what you describe. Do you remember which gear you were in?

**Sven:** The top gear, I think. I had been riding on the flat.

**Callum:** That is the usual cause. Starting up a steep slope in a high gear asks the motor for a lot of current at low
speed. Before the ramp, shift down to a low gear and use Haul, the strongest level, to get going. The manual also says to reduce the load or walk the bike
up if it still stalls.

**Sven:** How heavy were the crates?

**Callum:** If you are over about 200 kg in total, including yourself, you are over the limit. The rack is rated for 80 kg.

**Sven:** I think I was around 160 kg all up, so I'm within the limit.

**Callum:** Then the gear was the cause. If E15 appears on level ground, please write to us again.

## Resolution

Over-current protection triggered by starting a steep slope in a high gear with a heavy load. Advice: shift down early, use
Haul, stay within the 200 kg total load and 80 kg rack rating. No parts needed.

## Note for the support team

E15 on the Cargo 700 is usually a stall on a slope. Gear choice, not a fault.
