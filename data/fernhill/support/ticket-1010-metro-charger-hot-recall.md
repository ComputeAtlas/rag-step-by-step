## Customer's question

Jonas phoned: "The charger for my Metro 300 is too hot to touch after about two hours. Is that normal? I bought the bike
in April."

## Conversation

**Tom:** No, that is not normal. Please unplug it now. Can you read me the label on the charger? I need the part number
and the date code.

**Jonas:** It says FH-2310-A and next to the serial it says 0425.

**Tom:** Date code 0425 means April 2025. That charger is covered by our safety notice SN-2025-03. Chargers of part number
FH-2310-A with date codes from 0325 to 0625 can run too hot during long charging. We replace them free of charge, and you do
not need a receipt.

**Jonas:** Can I carry on using it until the new one arrives?

**Tom:** I would rather you did not. If you must charge, do it for short periods in the same room where you can watch
it, never overnight. I will arrange an FH-2310-B charger to be sent within five working days, with a prepaid label for the old one.

**Jonas:** Does this affect my warranty?

**Tom:** No. Using an affected charger before the notice does not affect your warranty, and a battery damaged by an affected charger is
covered under the warranty for the year you bought the bike.

*Follow-up, 29 July 2025.* New FH-2310-B charger delivered; the old charger was returned with the prepaid label.

## Resolution

Charger was an FH-2310-A with date code 0425, within safety notice SN-2025-03. Replaced free with an FH-2310-B. No effect on
the bike's warranty.

## Note for the support team

Date code 0325 to 0625 and part FH-2310-A means safety notice SN-2025-03. Send the free replacement FH-2310-B; no receipt needed.
