## Customer's question

Uma phoned: "My dealer fitted a new controller to my Cargo 700 yesterday. Now the display shows E14 at start-up. The dealer says it is not their
fault."

## Conversation

**Tom:** E14 means the display and the controller have different firmware versions. A new controller (FH-6252-A) arrives with the
latest firmware, and your display still has the old one. The two must carry the same version.

**Uma:** What version does the display show?

**Tom:** Hold Mode for two seconds and open Menu > Bike. It shows both build numbers.

**Uma:** The display is on D2.4.1 and the controller on C2.6.0.

**Tom:** That is the mismatch. v2.4 on the display and v2.6 on the controller. Update the bike to v2.6 with the Fernhill Service app. It
takes about 12 minutes and needs the battery above 50%. Both will then match.

*14 August 2026.* Uma updated the display. E14 is gone. The dealer apologised for not doing the update; it is part of fitting a controller.

## Resolution

Firmware mismatch (display D2.4.1, controller C2.6.0) after a controller was fitted. Updated both to v2.6 with the Service
app. No further parts needed.

## Note for the support team

After fitting a controller the display usually needs a firmware update to match (E14).
