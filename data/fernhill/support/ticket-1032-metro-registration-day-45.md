## Customer's question

Dmitri wrote: "I bought my Metro 300 on 2 February 2026 and only today read that registering gives a longer battery
warranty. It is day 45. Can I still register to get the 4 years?"

## Conversation

**Tom:** Thank you for asking, Dmitri. The 2026 policy says: "Register your bike within 30 days of purchase and your battery
warranty grows to 4 years." The 30 days ended on 4 March 2026, so I'm sorry to say the longer battery warranty is no
longer available for this bike. The standard cover still applies: 3 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first.

**Dmitri:** Is there any chance of an exception?

**Tom:** The registration bonus is set in the policy and we apply it the same way for everyone, so I cannot extend it. You can still
register the bike at fernhillbikes.example/register. It is useful because we will contact you about any safety notice or
important firmware change, but it will not change your warranty length.

**Dmitri:** Understood. Thanks for being straight with me.

## Resolution

Registration after 30 days does not extend the battery warranty. Bike remains covered by the 2026 standard terms
(3 years or 800 cycles). Customer registered anyway for safety notices.

## Note for the support team

The 30-day registration window cannot be extended. Register anyway to receive safety notices.
