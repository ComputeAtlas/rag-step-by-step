## Customer's question

Remy wrote: "My Cargo 700 arrived on 20 March. It is much longer and heavier than I imagined and I cannot get it into my
garage. I have ridden 40 km. Can I send it back?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** You are inside the 30 days, Remy (today is day 19), and you have ridden well under the 100 km limit, so you can return it for a full refund.
You said you bought it yourself, not through a business plan, so the standard returns policy applies. The collection fee for a
Cargo 700 is £150, because it goes by pallet, and it is deducted from the refund. Do you still have the crate?

**Remy:** Yes, I kept it as the delivery driver suggested.

**Hannah:** Good. Please repack the bike, with the charger, both keys, the tools and the manuals. Before the courier arrives, take the battery out and keep
it with the bike. I will book the pallet collection for you.

**Remy:** Does the £150 apply even though it's not faulty?

**Hannah:** Yes. The fee is waived only for a faulty bike, damage on delivery or an item sent in error. If you can take it to
a Fernhill store yourself, there is no fee, but this bike weighs 41.5 kg and needs two people to lift.

*23 April 2026.* The bike was collected on 10 April and checked. The refund, less the £150 collection fee, was paid on 23 April.

## Resolution

Return accepted within 30 days (19 days after delivery, 40 km ridden). Collection fee of £150 deducted (Cargo 700 pallet
collection). Refund paid within 10 working days of the bike being received.

## Note for the support team

Cargo 700 return collection fee is £150 (pallet). Return in person to a store to avoid it.
