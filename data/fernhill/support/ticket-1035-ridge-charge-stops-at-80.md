## Customer's question

Nikhil asked: "My charger turns green when the battery shows 80%. It used to go to 100%. Is the battery failing?"

## Conversation

**Isla:** That sounds like Battery care mode, Nikhil. It arrived in firmware v2.5 and stops charging at 80% to protect the cells.
When it is on, the charger light turns green at 80%. Did you update recently?

**Nikhil:** Yes, I updated to v2.6 last week.

**Isla:** Since v2.6 you can toggle the mode from the quick menu with a short press of the mode button and the plus button, so you may have switched it on by accident. To turn it off, go to
Menu > Battery and switch Battery care mode off, or use the quick menu again. Then charge once more.

**Nikhil:** I found it. It shows 100% now.

**Isla:** If you have a short commute, leaving it on is good for the battery. For long rides, charge to 100%.

## Resolution

Battery care mode (80% limit) had been switched on by accident. Customer switched it off. No fault.

## Note for the support team

Battery care mode stops charging at 80%. In v2.6 it can be toggled from the quick menu.
