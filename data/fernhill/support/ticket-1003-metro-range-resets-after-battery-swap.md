## Customer's question

Elin wrote: "Every time I take the battery indoors to charge and put it back, the display says the range is 100% again, even
when the battery was only half full. I nearly ran out of charge on a ride yesterday because I trusted it."

## Conversation

**Isla:** Thank you for reporting this, Elin. What firmware version does your bike show under Menu > Bike?

**Elin:** v2.3.

**Isla:** That matches a known issue. On the Metro 300 the range estimate resets after the battery is removed and refitted. It is
listed as a known issue in the release notes from v2.0 onwards. The battery bars themselves are correct, so please trust
the bars, not the range figure, until it is fixed. The fix is in firmware v2.4, which is due in the first week of April.

**Elin:** Will I have to go to a dealer?

**Isla:** No. You can update with the Fernhill Service app. It takes about 12 minutes and the battery must be above 50%.

*10 April 2025.*

**Elin:** I installed v2.4 yesterday. I removed the battery twice today and the estimate stayed where it was. Thank you.

**Isla:** Great. I will close the ticket.

## Resolution

Known issue in firmware v2.3 and earlier on the Metro 300 (range estimate reset after a battery swap), fixed in v2.4,
released on 3 April 2025. Customer updated with the Fernhill Service app and confirmed the fix.

## Note for the support team

Known issue listed in the release notes for v2.0 to v2.3. The fix shipped in v2.4 (3 April 2025).
