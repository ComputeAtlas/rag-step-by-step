## Customer's question

Greta wrote: "Last summer I could do 55 km on my Metro 300 in Tour. This month I only get about 36 km. Has the battery worn out?"

## Conversation

**Hannah:** It is unlikely, Greta. Battery health is shown under Menu > Battery. What does it say?

**Greta:** 96%.

**Hannah:** That is excellent, and the cycle count? 

**Greta:** 214.

**Hannah:** So the battery is healthy. Two things take distance off in winter. First, cold: near 0 °C the range falls by roughly a
quarter. Second, tyre pressure falls with the temperature. Could you check the pressure?

**Greta:** The tyres are at 2.0 bar.

**Hannah:** The Metro 300 wants 3.0 to 4.0 bar, and 2.0 bar is a lot of extra rolling resistance. Pump them to 3.5 bar and keep
the battery indoors until you leave.

**Greta:** Done. I got 47 km yesterday.

## Resolution

No battery fault (health 96% at 214 cycles). Loss of range caused by winter cold and tyre pressure of 2.0 bar instead of
3.0 to 4.0 bar. Advice given.

## Note for the support team

Check battery health and cycle count first. Healthy battery plus low range usually means cold or tyre pressure.
