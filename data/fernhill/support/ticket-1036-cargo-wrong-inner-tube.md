## Customer's question

Hana wrote: "I got a puncture on my Cargo 700 and ordered inner tube FH-4191-A because it said Presta valve and 'Fernhill' in the
title. It is far too small for the wheel."

## Conversation

**Rhys:** I'm sorry, Hana. FH-4191-A is the 28-inch tube for the Metro 300. The Cargo 700 has 24 x 2.6 in tyres, and its tube
is FH-4192-A at £9. The catalogue lists the wheel size beside each number, but it is easy to miss.

**Hana:** Can I swap it?

**Rhys:** Yes, unused parts can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Send the FH-4191-A tube back in its packaging (£7), and we
will refund it. Order the FH-4192-A at £9. For tyre pressure, run 2.5 bar unloaded or 3.2 bar loaded; the maximum on the sidewall is
3.5 bar.

**Hana:** I will also buy a puncture kit.

**Rhys:** The kit is FH-4199-A at £6.50 and suits every model.

*4 May 2026.* The correct tube arrived and was fitted. The wrong tube was returned.

## Resolution

Wrong inner tube ordered (FH-4191-A for Metro 300 instead of FH-4192-A for Cargo 700). Customer returned the unused tube
within 14 days and bought the correct one at £9. A tube is a wear part, not covered by the warranty.

## Note for the support team

Tube part numbers: FH-4190-A (29 in), FH-4191-A (28 in), FH-4192-A (24 in).
