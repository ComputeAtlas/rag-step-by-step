## Customer's question

Lena wrote: "Since I updated to v2.5 the display flickers on my evening commute, between bright and dim, over and over. It
is very distracting."

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Thanks for the report, Lena. This is the known issue in the v2.5 release notes: auto-brightness on the Dash 2
can flicker at dusk when the light level hovers around the switching point. The workaround is to open Menu > Display >
Brightness and set it to Manual. A permanent fix is being prepared for the next release.

**Lena:** I set it to Manual. It is stable now.

**Hannah:** I will keep this ticket open until the fix is out and ask you to update.

*20 January 2026.* **Hannah:** Firmware v2.6 was released on 12 January 2026 and fixes the auto-brightness flicker. You can
update with the Fernhill Service app and switch brightness back to Auto if you like.

**Lena:** Updated and set to Auto. No flicker at dusk. Thanks. I also noticed the display now turns itself off after
5 minutes.

**Hannah:** Yes, v2.6 changed auto-off from 10 minutes to 5.

## Resolution

Known issue in firmware v2.5 (Dash 2 auto-brightness flicker). Workaround in September 2025: manual brightness. Fixed by
firmware v2.6 on 12 January 2026; customer updated and confirmed.

## Note for the support team

Auto-brightness flicker was fixed in v2.6. Until then, manual brightness is the workaround.
