## Customer's question

Viktor called: "My Ridge 500 brakes started squealing loudly after the last two wet rides. They still stop the bike, but
the noise is embarrassing. Do I need new pads?"

## Conversation

**Rhys:** Probably not yet. How far have you ridden?

**Viktor:** About 380 km.

**Rhys:** That is within the first pad check at 500 km. Squeal in wet weather usually comes from water or grease on the
rotors or glazed pads. Did you clean the chain recently with a spray or lubricant?

**Viktor:** Yes, I lubricated the chain last weekend.

**Rhys:** Lubricant on a rotor is a very common cause. Take the wheels out, wipe the rotors with brake cleaner or isopropyl
alcohol, and let them dry. Then bed the pads in again: ten firm stops from walking pace. If it still squeals, look at the
pads. Replace them when 1.5 mm of material is left. The Ridge 500 uses FH-3105-A.

**Viktor:** What if they are fine but still noisy?

**Rhys:** Then ask a dealer to check the caliper alignment at the free first check. The first check is at 100 km or four
weeks, which you have passed, so a normal service will do it.

**Viktor called back two days later:** The noise has gone after cleaning. Thanks.

## Resolution

Contaminated rotors from chain lubricant. Advice given: clean rotors, bed in pads, replace pads (part FH-3105-A) at 1.5 mm.
No parts sent.

## Note for the support team

Noise in the wet: clean the rotors first, then re-bed the pads. Pad thickness limit is 1.5 mm.
