## Customer's question

Gus asked: "My Metro 300 has had four punctures since September. Are the tyres bad?"

## Conversation

**Tom:** The Metro 300 has puncture-resistant tyres, part FH-4120-A, so four in three months suggests something else. What pressure do
you run?

**Gus:** I do not know. They look okay. I never check.

**Tom:** Please borrow a pump with a gauge. The Metro 300 manual says 3.0 to 4.0 bar for normal commuting, 4.5 bar maximum.

**Gus:** I just did. The front is 1.8 bar and the rear is 2.0.

**Tom:** That is the cause. A tyre at 2 bar is soft enough for the rim to pinch the tube on a kerb. Pump to 3.5 bar, and check weekly,
because a puncture-resistant tyre still loses air slowly. If the problem returns, run your fingers inside the tyre for
a thorn or a sliver of glass.

**Gus:** I will check every Sunday.

**Tom:** Replacement tubes for the 28 inch wheel are FH-4191-A at £7, and the repair kit is FH-4199-A at £6.50.

## Resolution

Low tyre pressure (1.8 to 2.0 bar instead of 3.0 to 4.0 bar). Advice given. Tyres and tubes are wear parts and not covered by
the warranty.

## Note for the support team

Metro 300 tyres run 3.0 to 4.0 bar. Low pressure causes most repeat punctures.
