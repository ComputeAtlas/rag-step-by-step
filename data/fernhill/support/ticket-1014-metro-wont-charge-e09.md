## Customer's question

Liam phoned: "I left my Metro 300 in the shed for three months. Now the display shows E09 and the charger light stays red but
nothing seems to happen."

## Conversation

**Rhys:** E09 on the Metro 300 means the battery voltage is too low. After a long time at low charge the pack can fall below its
protection voltage. The red light on the charger is normal while it works. Did the battery have charge when you put it
away?

**Liam:** I think it was around 10%.

**Rhys:** That is the cause. The manual says to store the battery at 40 to 60% and top it up every three months. Here is what to
do. Bring the battery indoors, so it is above 5 °C. Connect the charger and leave it for 30 minutes. A deeply
discharged pack can take that long to start charging.

**Liam:** It is still on E09 after 20 minutes.

**Rhys:** Be patient for another ten minutes. If the charger light is still red but the battery percentage starts to rise, it
has started.

*16 September 2025.* Liam called back: the battery began charging after about 28 minutes and reached 100%. Health test
at the dealer was not needed.

## Resolution

Deeply discharged pack after storage at about 10%. Recovered by charging for 30 minutes with the supplied charger. Customer
advised to store at 40 to 60% and check every 3 months. A pack that does not recover would need a health test.

## Note for the support team

E09 after storage: leave the charger connected for 30 minutes. Remind the customer about storage at 40 to 60%.
