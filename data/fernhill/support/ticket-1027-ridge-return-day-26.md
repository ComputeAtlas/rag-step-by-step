## Customer's question

Quinn wrote: "My Ridge 500 was delivered on 28 January. I have decided it is too much bike for me. Today is day 26. Can I
return it? I have ridden it for around 85 km."

## Conversation

**Isla:** Yes, you are inside the 30 days. You can return a new bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund if
it has been ridden no more than 100 km, it is clean and complete, and it goes back in the original box. At 85 km you are under the limit.

**Quinn:** I still have the box. Is there a fee?

**Isla:** A collection fee of £75 is deducted from the refund for the Ridge 500. If you take the bike to a Fernhill store yourself there is
no fee; the nearest to you is Stonehallow, open Tuesday to Saturday. The refund then comes within 10 working days of us receiving the bike.

**Quinn:** I'll take it to Stonehallow on Friday.

*9 March 2026.* The bike was returned in good condition on Friday 27 February, day 30. The full price was refunded to the original card
on 9 March with no deduction.

## Resolution

Return accepted within the 30-day window (85 km ridden, limit 100 km). Customer returned the bike in person to the
Stonehallow Store, so no collection fee applied. Full refund issued.

## Note for the support team

Returns: 30 days from delivery, no more than 100 km. In-person returns to a store carry no fee.
