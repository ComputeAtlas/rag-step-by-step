## Customer's question

Priya wrote in chat: "My display shows E03 and the motor does nothing. It happens when I switch the bike on while standing
with one foot on the pedal."

## Conversation

**Hannah:** Hi Priya. On the Ridge 500, E03 means the torque sensor signal is out of range. The sensor in the motor reads
how hard you push the pedals, and if you press on a pedal at the moment of switch-on, it sets its zero point wrongly.

**Priya:** So nothing is broken?

**Hannah:** Most likely not. Switch the bike off, wait ten seconds, and switch it on again with your weight off the
pedals. Keep the bike upright, with the cranks level and your foot on the ground.

**Priya:** I tried it. The code is gone and the motor works.

**Hannah:** Good. If E03 comes back even when you are not touching the pedals, write to us again and we will arrange a
dealer visit. By the way, on the Metro 300 the same code E03 means something different: a missing cadence sensor signal.
The tables in each manual are specific to the model.

## Resolution

Torque sensor zeroed while the pedal was loaded at switch-on. Cleared by restarting without pedal pressure. No parts
needed.

## Note for the support team

E03 means different things on different models. Always check which model before advising.
