## Notes

1. The Ridge 500 accessory port supplies up to 6 W. A bigger light draws too much and shows E13.
2. A child seat must be fitted by a Fernhill dealer or with the dealer fitting sheet. Up to two seats may be fitted on the
   Cargo 700, each for a child of up to 22 kg. Children must wear helmets.
3. The Cargo 700 rack is rated for 80 kg and the bike for 200 kg in total, including you and any children.
4. Do not fit a rack to a Ridge 500; it is not designed for one.
5. Fitting a part of the wrong model can damage the bike. Check the compatibility column before you order.
