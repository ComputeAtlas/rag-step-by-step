### Choosing the right battery

- The numbers 2291 and 2219 are easy to mix up. **2291 is Ridge 500. 2219 is Metro 300.** The two packs have different
  capacities (504 Wh and 324 Wh) and different shapes.
- The letter A or B on a Ridge 500 or Metro 300 battery tells you which type you have. A B pack in a bike that has
  not been updated shows error E01. Update the firmware to v2.3 or later and the pack works. A pack of type A works
  in every Ridge 500 and Metro 300 regardless of firmware.
- The Cargo 700 uses a 48 V pack. It is not interchangeable with the 36 V packs.
- Never mix batteries between models, even if a pack seems to slide in.

### Battery warranty and price

A battery that fails inside its warranty period is replaced free. The period depends on the year you bought the bike
(see the 2026 and 2025 warranty policies). Outside the warranty the prices above apply. A Fernhill dealer can test a
battery's health in about 15 minutes; the test is part of every service.

### Why the charger matters

- A 36 V charger plugged into the Cargo 700 triggers error E10, and a 48 V charger must never be used on a 36 V battery.
- A charger bought from Fernhill with the correct part number is the same as the one supplied with the bike, so using it
  does not affect the warranty. A charger from another maker does.
- Charging times with the supplied charger: Ridge 500, 5 h (0 to 100%); Metro 300, 3 h 30 min; Cargo 700, 4 h 30 min.
