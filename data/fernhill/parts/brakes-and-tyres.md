# Parts Catalogue: Brakes and Tyres

Prices in pounds, including VAT, from 1 January 2026. Pads, tyres and tubes are wear parts and are not covered by the
warranty.

## Brake pads

| Part number | Description | Fits | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| FH-3105-A | Brake pads, 4-piston caliper, organic compound (set for one wheel) | Ridge 500, front and rear | £24 |
| FH-3105-B | Brake pads, 4-piston caliper, sintered heavy-duty compound (set for one wheel) | Cargo 700, front and rear | £29 |
| FH-3110-A | Brake pads, 2-piston caliper, organic compound (set for one wheel) | Metro 300, front and rear | £19 |

Notes:

- FH-3105-A and FH-3105-B fit the same caliper shape, but they are different compounds. Fitting the organic pad to a
  Cargo 700 is unsafe because the pad overheats under a heavy load. Fitting the sintered pad to a Ridge 500 works, but
  is noisy and wears the rotors.
- Replace pads when 1.5 mm of material is left. Checking intervals are in each owner's manual: every 500 km on the
  Ridge 500, 750 km on the Metro 300 and 400 km on the Cargo 700.
- After fitting new pads, bed them in with 10 firm stops from walking pace.

## Common ordering mistakes

1. Ordering FH-3105-A for a Cargo 700 because it looks the same. The Cargo 700 needs FH-3105-B.
2. Ordering a front tyre (FH-4101-A) twice for a Ridge 500 when the rear (FH-4101-B) is the worn one.
3. Ordering a 160 mm rotor (FH-3206-A) for a Cargo 700; its front rotor is 203 mm (FH-3207-A).
