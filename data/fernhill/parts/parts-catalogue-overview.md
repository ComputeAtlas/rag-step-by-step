# Fernhill Parts Catalogue 2026: Overview and How to Read Part Numbers

Edition January 2026. Prices are in pounds sterling, include VAT at 20% and apply from 1 January 2026. The catalogue
is split into four files: batteries and chargers, brakes and tyres, displays and motors, and drivetrain, lights and
accessories.

## How a Fernhill part number is built

Every part has a number in the form `FH-NNNN-L`, for example `FH-2291-B`.

| Piece | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `FH` | Fernhill | FH |
| First two digits | Category | 22 = battery packs, 23 = chargers, 31 = brake pads, 32 = brake rotors, 33 = brake levers, 41 = tyres and tubes, 51 = displays and controls, 61 = motors, 62 = controllers and sensors, 71 = drivetrain, 82 = racks and stands, 81 = lights, 83 to 88 = other accessories, 90 = service kits |
| Last two digits | Item within the category | 91 = Ridge 500 battery |
| Letter | Revision or variant | B |

The final letter is not always "newer". It means different things in different categories:

- For batteries and chargers the letter is a **revision**: -B replaces -A (FH-2291-B is the newer Ridge 500 battery).
- For brake pads and tyres the letter marks a **variant**: FH-3105-A is the Ridge 500 pad and FH-3105-B is the heavy-duty
  Cargo 700 pad. FH-4101-A is a front tyre and FH-4101-B is the matching rear tyre. Neither replaces the other.
- Digits can look alike. FH-2291-B is a Ridge 500 battery and FH-2219-B is a Metro 300 battery. FH-2310-B is a 36 V
  charger and FH-2320-A is a 48 V charger. Always check the whole number and the model it fits.

## Ordering

1. Find your bike's model and serial number (stamped under the frame, next to the pedals). The serial number tells you
   the model: `FH-R5-` is a Ridge 500, `FH-M3-` a Metro 300 and `FH-C7-` a Cargo 700.
2. Order at fernhillbikes.example/parts, by calling 01632 960 410, or through any Fernhill dealer.
3. Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are packed the same day. Delivery terms are in the shipping and
   delivery document.

Parts that fail inside the warranty period are replaced free through a warranty claim; do not order them in that case.
Wear parts (pads, chains, tyres, tubes, cassettes and grips) are never covered by the warranty.

## Support parts for old bikes

Fernhill keeps spare parts for every model for at least seven years after the last bike of that model is sold.
Parts that have been replaced by a newer revision remain on sale while stock lasts, and the catalogue lists which bikes
they fit.

## Returns on parts

Unused parts in their original packaging can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Batteries that have been charged
or fitted cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Full terms are in the returns policy.

## Fitting

Most parts can be fitted at home with the tools in the owner's manual. Motors, controllers and brake bleeding should be
done by a Fernhill dealer. After fitting a controller or display, update the firmware so that both carry the same version
(error E14 appears otherwise).

## Frequently asked about parts

**Do I need my serial number?** For batteries, controllers, displays and motors, yes: the serial number tells us which version of the part fits.
**Can I order a part for a bike bought in 2022?** Yes, we keep spare parts for at least seven years after the last bike of a model is sold.
**Are parts covered by a warranty?** A part bought from Fernhill carries the usual legal guarantee. A part fitted under a warranty claim is covered
for the rest of the original period.
