# Parts Catalogue: Drivetrain, Lights and Accessories

Prices in pounds, including VAT, from 1 January 2026. Chains, cassettes and grips are wear parts and are not covered
by the warranty.

## Drivetrain

| Part number | Description | Fits | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| FH-7101-A | Chain, 12-speed | Ridge 500 | £34 |
| FH-7102-A | Chain, hub-gear type | Metro 300 | £19 |
| FH-7103-A | Chain, 9-speed, reinforced | Cargo 700 | £27 |
| FH-7150-A | Cassette, 12-speed | Ridge 500 | £89 |
| FH-7151-A | Cassette, 9-speed | Cargo 700 | £46 |
| FH-7301-A | Pedals, pair | All models | £22 |

Replace the chain at 0.5% stretch on the Ridge 500 and Cargo 700, and at 0.75% on the Metro 300. A worn chain wears
the cassette; fitting a new chain onto a worn cassette causes skipping.
