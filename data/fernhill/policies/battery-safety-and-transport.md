# Fernhill Bikes: Battery Safety, Storage, Transport and Recycling

Applies to the Ridge 500 (504 Wh), Metro 300 (324 Wh) and Cargo 700 (720 Wh) batteries. Edition January 2026.

## Safe charging

- Use only the charger supplied with your bike or an identical replacement from Fernhill (see the parts catalogue).
- Charge indoors, on a hard, flat, non-flammable surface, away from curtains, bedding and exits.
- Fit a working smoke alarm in the room where you charge.
- Do not charge a battery that is wet, damaged, swollen or hot. Let a hot battery cool to room temperature first.
- Charge at temperatures between 5 and 40 °C. Do not charge a frozen battery.
- Unplug the charger when the light turns green. The charger stops automatically, but there is no reason to leave it
  connected for days.
- Never cover the charger or battery while charging.

## What to do if a battery is damaged

Signs of a damaged battery: swelling, dents, a sweet or chemical smell, hissing, unusual heat when idle, discolouration
or leaking liquid.

1. Stop using and charging it.
2. If it is safe to do so, take it outside and put it on stone or concrete, away from buildings.
3. If it is smoking or hot, keep away from it and call the emergency services.
4. Contact support@fernhillbikes.example. We replace a battery that was damaged by a fault, not by a crash.

## Storage

| Length | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Up to 2 weeks | Leave at any charge above 20% |
| More than 2 weeks | Store at 40 to 60% charge, indoors, at 10 to 20 °C |
| A whole season | Check every 3 months and top up to 50 to 60% |

A battery stored empty can fall below its protection voltage and may not charge again (error E09).

## Transporting

- **Car:** remove the battery before putting the bike on an exterior rack, and do not leave it in a hot car.
- **Train and ferry:** operators set their own rules for e-bikes and batteries. Ask before you travel.
- **Air:** all three Fernhill batteries are above 160 Wh, so none of them can be carried on a passenger flight.
- **Post:** never post a battery in an ordinary parcel. Fernhill ships batteries as dangerous goods by road only (see
  shipping and delivery).

## End of life

A battery that holds less than 70% of its original capacity is near the end of its useful life for most riders. Inside
the warranty period, falling below that level counts as a defect under the 2026 warranty policy (below 60% under the
2025 policy). Read the warranty policy for the year you bought your bike.

When a battery is finished:

1. Never put it in household waste or a normal recycling bin.
2. Take it to any Fernhill dealer. Recycling of Fernhill batteries is free, whether or not you buy a new one.
3. Tape over the contacts if the battery is damaged.

Fernhill's recycling partner recovers about 90% of the weight of each pack as metal and plastic.
