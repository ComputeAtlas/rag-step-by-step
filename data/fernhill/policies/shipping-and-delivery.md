# Fernhill Bikes: Shipping and Delivery

Effective 1 January 2026. Our courier partner for bikes is Northway Freight. Parts go by standard parcel service.

## Bike delivery

| Model | Delivery time (mainland UK) | Delivered as |
|---|---|---|
| Ridge 500 | 3 to 5 working days | Boxed, 29 kg |
| Metro 300 | 3 to 5 working days | Boxed, 24 kg |
| Cargo 700 | 7 to 10 working days (built to order) | Crated on a pallet, 49 kg |

- Delivery of bikes to mainland UK addresses is free.
- The courier sends a text on the morning of delivery with a two-hour time window.
- Someone aged 18 or over must be at home to sign.
- The Cargo 700 comes on a pallet and needs two people to lift. The driver does not carry it into the house.
- Bikes arrive 95% assembled. Set-up takes 20 to 45 minutes (see the quick-start guide). If you prefer, Fernhill dealers
  offer a set-up service (see service and repair pricing).

### Surcharges and other destinations

| Destination | Surcharge | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| Mainland UK | Free | As above |
| Scottish Highlands and islands | £49 | add 2 to 3 working days |
| Northern Ireland | £79 | 5 to 7 working days |
| Republic of Ireland | £99 | 7 to 10 working days |
| Netherlands and Germany | £119 | 10 to 14 working days |

We do not currently ship bikes outside the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands and Germany.

## Missed or failed deliveries

The courier makes two delivery attempts. After a second failed attempt the bike returns to our depot and you can ask
for a third delivery for £35.

## Damage and missing items

1. Check the box before the driver leaves. If it is badly damaged, write it on the delivery note.
2. Report any damage within **48 hours of delivery** to orders@fernhillbikes.example, with photos of the box and the
   damage.
3. If a bike or part has not arrived 5 working days after the latest date we gave you, contact us.

We repair or replace anything damaged in transit at no cost to you, and collection of a damaged bike is free.

## Parts delivery

| Order | Charge |
|---|---|
| Parts order under £50 | £4.95 |
| Parts order of £50 or more | Free |
| Battery pack | £15 flat rate |
| Next-day delivery (not for batteries) | £9.95 |

- Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are packed the same day. Standard parts arrive in 2 to 3 working days.
- Lithium-ion batteries are shipped as dangerous goods. They go by road only, never by air, and take 3 to 5 working
  days. A signature is required.
- Parts are delivered to the address on the order. We cannot deliver to a parcel locker.

## Delivery to a dealer

You can have your bike delivered to a participating Fernhill dealer for free, and the dealer will set it up for you. The
dealer can offer the set-up service at the price listed in service and repair pricing.

## Questions about delivery

**Can I choose the delivery day?** You can choose a delivery day when you place the order if the date is at least 3 working days away.
The courier then confirms a two-hour window by text on the morning of delivery.

**Can I change the delivery address after ordering?** Yes, until the order is dispatched. Write to orders@fernhillbikes.example.

**Can someone else receive the bike?** Anyone aged 18 or over can sign for it, but a Cargo 700 pallet needs two people to move.

**What if the bike is delivered to my flat on a high floor?** The driver delivers to the door of the building or the
ground-floor entrance. Carrying the box upstairs is not part of the service.

**Does the courier take the packaging away?** The courier will take away the Cargo 700 crate if you ask when you sign for the delivery. Ridge 500 and
Metro 300 boxes should be kept for 30 days in case you wish to return the bike.

**Will I be able to track my order?** Yes. You receive a tracking link in the dispatch confirmation email.

**What are the working days?** Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
