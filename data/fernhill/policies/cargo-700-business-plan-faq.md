# Cargo 700 Business Plan: Practical Questions

Edition February 2026. A companion to the Cargo 700 business plan document. Questions to business@fernhillbikes.example.

## Setting up the depot

### How many bikes can I charge at once?

Each Cargo 700 charger (FH-2320-A) draws up to 220 W from the wall. A standard UK 13 A socket supplies about 3 kW, but we advise
no more than 8 chargers per socket circuit, and we recommend a separate circuit for a fleet of more than 20 bikes. Charge in a
dry room with a working smoke alarm, on a non-flammable surface, as described in the battery safety document.

### How long does a battery take to charge between shifts?

From empty to full in 4 hours 30 minutes, and to 80% in 3 hours. Many fleets use Battery care mode (the 80% limit) so that bikes
are ready after a 3-hour break and the batteries last longer.

### Do I need a spare battery?

Not usually. A Cargo 700 does up to 70 km fully loaded in Eco and 100 km unloaded. Routes above 60 km a day can be split with a
mid-shift top-up. Spare batteries (FH-2292-A) cost £599, or £499 on a business plan.

## Running the fleet

### What does the fleet dashboard show?

For each bike: distance, charge cycles, battery health and the error codes seen that week. A bike near the 800-cycle limit
or with battery health falling to 70% is highlighted so that you can plan the replacement.

### What if a rider is heavier than usual or the load is too heavy?

The Cargo 700 is rated for 200 kg in total, including the rider, and the rack for 80 kg. Going above this is misuse. Bikes
that are overloaded can fail brakes or frames and are not covered by the warranty.

### How often should the bikes be serviced?

Every 1,500 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. With Fleet Care the service is done at your depot. Without it, each service
costs £139 at a Fernhill service centre or dealer.

### Can riders use their own charger?

No. Use the Fernhill FH-2320-A. A 36 V charger will show E10 and a charger from another maker can void the battery warranty.

### What about firmware?

Keep every bike on the latest firmware (v2.6 at the time of writing). Display and controller must match, or E14 appears. Updating
takes about 12 minutes per bike with the Fernhill Service app.

## Contract questions

### Can I add or remove bikes?

You can add bikes at the tier price for your new total fleet size. If the total fleet moves into a cheaper tier, new bikes get the
new price, and existing bikes keep their original price until renewal.

### Is insurance included?

No. You are responsible for insuring the bikes against theft and damage.

### Can I put my company logo on the bikes?

Yes. A custom colour wrap costs £85 per bike and adds about 2 weeks to the delivery time.

### What happens at the end of 36 months?

You can buy each bike for £1,150, return it, or renew the plan. If you buy it, the warranty continues for its original period,
but delivery and rental use is no longer covered once the bike is off the plan.

### What if I need to cancel before the bikes are built?

Free of charge until the build begins.
