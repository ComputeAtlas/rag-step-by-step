# Fernhill Business Plan for Cargo 700 Fleets

Effective 1 January 2026. For businesses that want three or more Cargo 700 bikes for deliveries, trades, school runs
or rental. Questions: business@fernhillbikes.example.

## Why a business plan exists

The standard warranty does not cover bikes used for rental or delivery work, because that use wears a bike far faster
than private riding. The 2026 warranty policy makes one exception: **a Cargo 700 bought on a Fernhill business plan is
covered for delivery and rental use.** A Cargo 700 bought any other way and then used for delivery or rental work is not
covered. (Under the 2025 warranty policy there was no exception at all, and the business plan did not exist.)

## Plan tiers

| Tier | Fleet size | Monthly price per bike | Term |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 3 to 9 bikes | £139 | 36 months |
| Fleet | 10 to 49 bikes | £129 | 36 months |
| Enterprise | 50 or more bikes | £119 | 36 months |

- Prices include VAT, the charger, two battery keys and the bike's delivery to one address.
- The list price of a single Cargo 700 bought outright is £4,499. At the end of the 36-month term you can keep a bike
  for a one-off payment of £1,150, return it, or renew the plan for a new bike.
- Payments are monthly by direct debit, in advance.
- The minimum order is 3 bikes. You can add bikes to a plan later at the same tier price.

## What is included

1. **Warranty** on the same terms as the warranty policy for the year the bikes were bought. Under the 2026 policy that
   means 2 years on the motor and controller, 3 years or 800 charge cycles on the battery, and 4 years on the battery if
   each bike is registered within 30 days. Register every bike at fernhillbikes.example/register.
2. **Rental and delivery use** is covered, as explained above.
3. **Fleet dashboard.** A weekly report per bike of distance, charge cycles, battery health and error codes.
4. **Rider training.** A one-hour session for your riders, at your depot or at a Fernhill store (up to 12 riders).
5. **Battery replacement price.** If a battery reaches its 800-cycle limit or falls below 70% of its original
   capacity after the warranty has ended, plan customers pay £499 for a new FH-2292-A instead of the catalogue price of £599.

The service itself, outside the add-on, costs £139 per visit (see service and repair pricing).

## Delivery

Fleet orders are built together. Delivery to one address takes 6 to 8 weeks from the signed agreement. Bikes arrive
crated on pallets, as in the standard delivery terms. Each pallet needs a forklift or two people to unload. Bikes with a
custom colour wrap (£85 per bike) can add 2 weeks.

## Responsibilities

- You are responsible for insuring the bikes against theft and damage.
- You must not exceed the 200 kg maximum total load or the 80 kg rack rating.
- Riders must follow the owner's manual. Crashes, misuse and riding through deep water are not covered.
- Charge the batteries with the Fernhill 48 V charger (FH-2320-A) supplied with each bike.
- Modifying the motor or controller voids the warranty on that bike.

## Ending the plan early

If you end the plan before the 36 months, you pay an early termination fee equal to 3 monthly payments per bike, and
return the bikes to our depot at our cost.

## What happens to the warranty if I buy the bike at the end?

The warranty continues for the rest of its original period. Delivery or rental use is still covered only while the bike
is on a Fernhill business plan. Once you own it outright, further delivery or rental use is no longer covered.

## Returns

Business-plan Cargo 700 bikes are made to order and cannot be returned under the standard returns policy. You can cancel
free of charge until the build begins.

## Worked examples

**A bakery orders 4 bikes.** Starter tier: 4 x £139 = £556 per month for 36 months, a total of £20,016 before any Fleet Care.

**A courier firm orders 12 bikes with Fleet Care.** Fleet tier: 12 x £129 = £1,548 per month, plus Fleet Care at 12 x £12 = £144 per month,
£1,692 per month in all.

**A city operator orders 50 bikes.** Enterprise tier: 50 x £119 = £5,950 per month for 36 months.

**A bike's battery reaches 800 cycles after the warranty.** A replacement FH-2292-A costs £499 for plan customers.

**A rider is injured in a crash.** The warranty does not cover crash damage. The customer insures the bikes.
