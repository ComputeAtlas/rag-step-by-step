# Safety Notice SN-2025-03: Chargers Supplied With Some Ridge 500 and Metro 300 Bikes

Issued 8 July 2025. Updated 12 January 2026.

## Summary

Some FH-2310-A chargers (36 V, 3 A) made between **3 March and 27 June 2025** can overheat during long charging. We
are replacing them **free of charge**. If your charger is affected, stop using it now.

## Which chargers are affected

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Part number | FH-2310-A |
| Date code (printed on the label next to the serial number) | 0325, 0425, 0525 and 0625 (that is, March to June 2025) |
| Supplied with | Ridge 500 and Metro 300 bikes sold from March to early July 2025 |
| Number of chargers made in that period | 3,862 |

Chargers with other date codes are not affected. The charger supplied with every Ridge 500 and Metro 300 since
30 June 2025 is the revised FH-2310-B and is not part of this notice. Cargo 700 chargers (FH-2320-A) are not affected.

## What went wrong

A component in the output stage of some chargers can run hotter than designed when the charger is used for several hours
at a time. In 14 reports the charger casing became too hot to touch, and in 2 of those the casing softened. No injuries
have been reported. No damage to batteries or bikes has been found.

## What to do

1. Look at the label on your charger. If the part number is FH-2310-A and the date code is 0325 to 0625, stop using it.
2. Go to fernhillbikes.example/charger-recall, write to safety@fernhillbikes.example, or ask any Fernhill dealer.
   You need your name, address and the number on the charger label. You do not need a receipt.
3. We send an FH-2310-B charger within 5 working days at no cost, with a prepaid label to return the old charger.
4. Do not throw the old charger away. Return it to us or a Fernhill dealer.

While you wait for the new charger, you may charge the battery for short periods while watching it, but do not leave it
charging unattended and do not charge overnight.

## Warranty

Using an affected charger before you received this notice does not affect your warranty. A battery damaged by an affected
charger is covered by the warranty for the year you bought the bike.

## Update, 12 January 2026

As of this date, 3,104 of the affected chargers have been replaced. The replacement programme remains open.
