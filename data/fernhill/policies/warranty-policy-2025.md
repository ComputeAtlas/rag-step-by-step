# Fernhill Bikes: Warranty Policy (2025 version)

Effective 1 January 2025. This policy applies to every Fernhill bike bought new between 1 January 2025 and
31 December 2025. It was replaced on 1 January 2026 by a new policy that applies to bikes bought from that date.

**Which policy applies to me?** The one in force on the day you bought the bike, as shown on your receipt. A bike bought
on 31 December 2025 stays under this 2025 policy for its whole warranty, even though the new policy started the next
day. Bikes bought before 1 January 2025 are covered by the terms in force on their purchase date.

## What is covered

| Part | How long |
|---|---|
| Frame | 5 years, for the original owner |
| Motor and controller | 2 years |
| Battery | 2 years or 500 full charge cycles, whichever comes first |
| Display, lights and wiring | 1 year |
| Paint and finish | 1 year |

All periods start on the date of purchase.

A battery counts as defective if it holds less than 60% of its original capacity while it is still under warranty.

A full charge cycle means the battery has delivered a total amount of energy equal to its full capacity. Two top-ups
of 50% count as one cycle. Your bike's display shows the count under Menu > Battery.

**Registering your bike is optional.** It lets us contact you about safety notices, but it does not extend any
warranty period. There is no registration bonus under this policy.

## What is not covered

- Parts that wear out with normal riding: tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes and grips.
- Damage from crashes, misuse, or riding through water deeper than the bottom of the motor.
- Batteries charged with any charger other than the Fernhill charger supplied with the bike or an identical replacement
  bought from Fernhill.
- Any bike used for rental or delivery work. There are no exceptions to this rule under the 2025 policy.
- Bikes that have had the motor or controller modified or tuned.

## How to make a claim

1. Find your bike's serial number. It is stamped under the frame, next to the pedals, and starts with `FH-`.
2. Write to warranty@fernhillbikes.example with your order number, the serial number, your bike's cycle count if you
   are claiming for the battery, and a photo of the problem.
3. We reply within 5 working days. If we approve the claim, we repair or replace the part within 20 working days.

If a part is replaced under warranty, the replacement is covered for the rest of the original warranty period.

## Other points

- The warranty is for the original owner only.
- Fernhill may choose to repair or replace a faulty part.
- Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by this warranty.
- Postage for a part sent to us for inspection is paid by Fernhill when the claim is approved.

## Worked examples

**A battery at 62% after 400 cycles, bought in June 2025.** It is within 2 years and 500 cycles, and 62% is above the 60% line, so
the battery is not defective. It is not covered, although it has aged.

**A battery at 55% after 150 cycles, bought in May 2025.** Within 2 years and 500 cycles, and below 60%: defective. The battery is replaced
free of charge and the replacement is covered for the rest of the original period.

**A frame crack 3 years after purchase, bought in 2025.** The frame is covered for 5 years for the original owner, so the claim is accepted.

**A display fault 14 months after purchase, bought in 2025.** The display, lights and wiring are covered for 1 year under this policy,
so the claim is not covered. (Under the 2026 policy this period is 2 years.)

**A Cargo 700 used for a delivery round, bought in 2025.** Any bike used for rental or delivery work is excluded. This policy has no exceptions.

**A battery charged with a charger bought from another maker.** Not covered. A charger bought from Fernhill with the same part number as
the one supplied is accepted.
