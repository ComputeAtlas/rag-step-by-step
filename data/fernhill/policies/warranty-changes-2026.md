# What Changed in the 2026 Warranty

Published 3 November 2025. Fernhill's warranty policy changes for bikes bought on or after 1 January 2026. This page compares
the two versions. If this page and a policy ever disagree, the policy wins.

## Which policy applies to you?

The one in force on the date of purchase on your receipt:

- Bought between 1 January 2025 and 31 December 2025: the 2025 warranty policy (warranty-policy-2025).
- Bought on or after 1 January 2026: the 2026 warranty policy (warranty-policy).
- Bought before 2025: the terms in force when you bought the bike.

The new policy is not applied backwards. A bike bought on 31 December 2025 stays under the 2025 policy.

## The two policies side by side

| | 2025 policy | 2026 policy |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | 5 years, original owner | Lifetime, original owner |
| Motor and controller | 2 years | 2 years |
| Battery | 2 years or 500 cycles | 3 years or 800 cycles |
| Battery defective when it holds less than | 60% of original capacity | 70% of original capacity |
| Registration | Optional, no bonus | Within 30 days: battery warranty grows to 4 years |
| Display, lights and wiring | 1 year | 2 years |
| Paint and finish | 1 year | 1 year |
| Rental or delivery use | Never covered | Covered for a Cargo 700 on a Fernhill business plan |
| We reply to a claim within | 5 working days | 2 working days |
| Approved repair or replacement within | 20 working days | 10 working days |

## Why we changed it

- Customers told us that 500 cycles was too few for people who ride every day. Raising it to 800 cycles lines up with the
  way batteries are made now.
- A lifetime frame warranty is a promise that we make the frames to last.
- Registering a bike lets us warn owners quickly about safety notices, so we reward it.
- Businesses asked how they could use a Cargo 700 for deliveries. The business plan answers that.

## What did not change

- The covered parts list and the exclusions for wear parts (tyres, tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes, grips), crashes,
  misuse, and deep water.
- A replacement part is covered for the rest of the original period.
- Paint and finish are covered for 1 year.
- The claim address, warranty@fernhillbikes.example.
- The serial number position, under the frame next to the pedals.

## Common questions

**I bought my bike in December 2025. Can I move to the 2026 policy?** No. The policy depends on the purchase date.

**My 2025 battery has 65% health. Is it defective?** Under the 2025 policy, a battery is defective below 60%. The 70% rule applies
only to bikes bought from 1 January 2026.

**Is the 2025 frame warranty shorter?** Yes. It is 5 years for the 2025 policy, against a lifetime for the original owner under the
2026 policy.
