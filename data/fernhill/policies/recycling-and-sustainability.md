# Fernhill Bikes: Recycling and Sustainability

Edition January 2026.

## Batteries

Batteries are the part of an electric bike that needs the most care at the end of its life. At Fernhill:

- Every battery sold by Fernhill can be returned to any Fernhill dealer or store for free recycling, whether or not you buy a
  new battery.
- Our recycling partner recovers about 90% of the weight of each pack as metals and plastics.
- Battery packs are built so that a dealer can replace one without special tools, and the plug fittings are the same across a
  model's A and B packs.
- A battery that has fallen below 70% of its original capacity is often still useful for lighter use. Do not dispose of it in
  household waste.
- We replace damaged batteries only after they come back to us, so that none is lost.

## Chargers and electronics

Old chargers, displays and controllers go back to a Fernhill dealer too. A charger replaced under safety notice SN-2025-03
is returned with a prepaid label and recycled.

## Making things last

- We keep spare parts for at least seven years after the last bike of a model is sold.
- The parts catalogue lists part numbers and prices publicly so independent repairers can use them.
- Wear parts such as pads, chains and tyres are cheap and can be fitted at home.
- Motors can be exchanged. A returned motor earns the service exchange price: £349 for the Ridge 500 motor, £219 for the
  Metro 300 motor and £399 for the Cargo 700 motor.

## Packaging

The bike boxes are made of recycled cardboard, and the foam is replaced by corrugated card on the Ridge 500 and Metro 300.
Please keep the box for 30 days in case you need to return the bike, then recycle it with your cardboard. The Cargo 700 crate is
wood from a certified forest; the courier can take it back on request when you sign for the delivery.

## The factory

The Hollinwick factory uses electricity from a renewable tariff. Water used for frame cleaning is recycled on site. Offcuts of
aluminium go back to the supplier for remelting.
