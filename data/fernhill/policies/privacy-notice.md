# Fernhill Bikes: Privacy Notice

Effective 1 January 2026. Fernhill Bikes Ltd is responsible for the personal information described here.

## What we collect

| When | What |
|---|---|
| You buy a bike or part | Your name, delivery and billing address, email address, phone number, order number |
| You register your bike | Your serial number and the date you bought the bike |
| You contact support | The content of your messages and notes about the case. Phone calls are recorded for training and quality |
| You use the Fernhill Service app | Your firmware versions, error codes, charge cycle count and the bike's serial number. We do not collect your location |

## Why we use it

- To deliver your order, answer your questions and carry out warranty repairs.
- To contact you if there is a safety notice for your bike, such as a charger replacement.
- To improve the bikes, for example by finding the most common error codes.

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the courier delivering your order, our payment provider and the
dealer you choose.

## How long we keep it

- Order records: 7 years.
- Registration and warranty records: for the length of the longest warranty on your bike, plus 1 year.
- Support messages: 3 years.
- Call recordings: 6 months.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Write to privacy@fernhillbikes.example. We reply
within one month. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

## Cookies

The website uses a small number of cookies to keep your basket and to count visits. We do not use cookies to sell advertising. You can switch off
non-essential cookies in your browser.

## Children

Fernhill bikes are for riders under 16 only if an adult supervises. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Test rides for under-16s
need an adult present.

## Changes

We update this notice when our practices change. The date at the top shows when the current version started.
