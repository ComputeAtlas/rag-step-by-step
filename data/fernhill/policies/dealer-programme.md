# Fernhill Authorised Dealer Programme

Effective 1 January 2026. For bike shops that want to sell and service Fernhill bikes. Questions:
dealers@fernhillbikes.example.

## Dealer levels

| Level | What the dealer does | Training needed |
|---|---|---|
| Sales | Shows and sells bikes, parts and accessories, runs test rides and the free first check | Fernhill product course (1 day) |
| Service | Everything above, plus full services, firmware updates, brake work, battery health tests | Fernhill service course (2 days) |
| Master Service | Everything above, plus motor, controller and display replacement and warranty repairs | Fernhill master technician course (3 days) and a yearly refresh |

## Requirements

1. A shop open to the public at least five days a week.
2. At least one example of each of the Ridge 500, Metro 300 and Cargo 700 on show (Cargo 700 for dealers of Service level and above).
3. A technician trained at the right level for each service the shop offers.
4. A stock of fast-moving parts: pads, tyres, tubes, chains, chargers and batteries.
5. Use of the Fernhill Service app and diagnostic cable for firmware updates.

## What dealers receive

- Parts at trade price.
- Free delivery of bikes and parts on weekly orders.
- Training, manuals and a listing on the store locations page.
- Warranty repair payment: Fernhill pays dealers for warranty labour at the standard labour rate of £48 per hour.

## Dealer promises to customers

- Accept the return of an unridden new bike for at least 14 days.
- Carry out the first check (after 100 km or 4 weeks) free of charge.
- Pass customer safety notices on promptly.
- Recycle old batteries for free. Dealers hand returned batteries to Fernhill's recycling partner.

## How to apply

Write to dealers@fernhillbikes.example with your shop's name, address, years in business and the technicians' training.
A regional manager visits the shop before the agreement is signed. The agreement is for two years and renews yearly.

## Network size

At 1 January 2026 the network had 64 authorised dealers: 48 in the UK, 6 in Ireland, 5 in the Netherlands and 5 in
Germany.

## Training courses in detail

| Course | Length | Content | Cost to the dealer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fernhill product course | 1 day | The three models, spec tables, test-ride routine, selling the registration | Free |
| Fernhill service course | 2 days | Full service routine, torque values, brake bleed, firmware updates, battery health tests | £250 |
| Fernhill master technician course | 3 days | Motor, controller and display replacement, diagnostics, warranty procedure | £450 |

Courses run at the Hollinwick factory. Technicians must repeat a yearly refresher of half a day, which is free. A dealer whose last
trained technician leaves is downgraded to the highest level it can still cover until a new technician is trained.

## Warranty repairs by dealers

1. Take the bike in and record the fault, the serial number and the order number.
2. Write to warranty@fernhillbikes.example before replacing any motor, controller, display or battery.
3. For approved claims, Fernhill supplies the part and pays the labour at the standard rate.
4. Return the faulty part within 14 days.
5. The 2026 policy sets a 2 working day reply and a 10 working day fix; the 2025 policy sets 5 and 20 working days. Dealers must tell the
   customer which policy applies, based on the receipt.
