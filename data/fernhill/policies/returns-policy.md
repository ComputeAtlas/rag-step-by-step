# Fernhill Bikes: Returns Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. This policy covers goods bought directly from fernhillbikes.example. If you bought from an
authorised dealer, the dealer handles the return; Fernhill requires every dealer to accept the return of an unridden
bike for at least 14 days.

## Returning a bike

You can return a new bike within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund of the price you paid, as long as:

1. The bike has been ridden for no more than 100 km in total. The display shows this under Mode > Total distance.
2. It is clean, complete and undamaged, with the charger, keys, tools and manuals.
3. It goes back in its original box or crate. Please keep the packaging for the 30 days.
4. You tell us before you send it. Write to orders@fernhillbikes.example with your order number and the reason.

### Return costs

| Item | Collection fee |
|---|---|
| Ridge 500 or Metro 300 | £75 |
| Cargo 700 (pallet collection) | £150 |

We deduct the collection fee from your refund, unless the bike is faulty, was damaged on delivery or was sent in error,
in which case collection is free. If you take the bike back to a Fernhill store yourself, there is no fee.

### Refunds

We refund to the original payment method within **10 working days** of receiving the bike back and checking it. If the
bike has been damaged, ridden more than 100 km or is missing parts, we may deduct a fair amount for the loss in value.

## Returning parts and accessories

- Parts and accessories can be returned unused, in their packaging, within **14 days of delivery**.
- Batteries that have been charged or fitted cannot be returned, unless they are faulty.
- Wear parts that have been fitted (pads, chains, tyres) cannot be returned.
- You pay the return postage on parts unless they are faulty or were sent in error.

## What cannot be returned

- Cargo 700 bikes ordered on a Fernhill business plan, and any bike with a custom wrap or special configuration.
- Bikes that have been modified.
- Bikes used for rental or delivery work.

## Faulty goods

If your bike or part is faulty, you do not need to use the return window. Contact support@fernhillbikes.example and we
will repair, replace or refund according to your legal rights. A fault inside the warranty period is dealt with by the
warranty policy for the year you bought the bike.

## Exchanges

To swap a bike for another model, return the first bike under this policy and place a new order. We cannot reserve the
new bike's delivery slot while the return is processed. Exchanges for a different size of the same model are free of
the collection fee.

## Cancelling before delivery

You may cancel an order before it is dispatched for a full refund. Cargo 700 orders are built to order and can be
cancelled free of charge up to 5 working days after the order, or until the build starts, whichever is sooner.

## Common questions about returns

**Does the 100 km limit include the test ride?** Yes, it counts the whole distance on the display. Total distance is shown by
pressing Mode on the display.

**Can I return a bike I bought at a Fernhill store?** Yes, within the same 30 days, in person at the store where you bought it or at any other Fernhill store. No collection fee
applies when you take it in yourself.

**What if the bike is faulty on arrival?** Contact support@fernhillbikes.example straight away. Collection is free and you do not
pay any fee. We repair, replace or refund the bike.

**What happens to the box?** Please keep the box or crate for 30 days. If you return the bike without suitable packaging, we may
charge for a replacement box.

**How will I be refunded?** To the card or account you paid with, within 10 working days of us receiving and checking the bike.
Deposits on built-to-order Cargo 700 bikes are refunded in the same way if you cancel before the build starts.

**Does returning a bike affect my warranty?** No. A returned bike is no longer yours, so its warranty does not transfer. The new
bike you buy has its own warranty from the date you buy it.
