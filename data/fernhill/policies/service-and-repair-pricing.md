# Fernhill Service and Repair Pricing

Effective 1 January 2026. Prices include VAT. They apply at Fernhill service centres and participating authorised
dealers.

## Standard service

| Model | Service | Interval | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridge 500 | Full service, including battery health test and firmware check | 2,000 km or 12 months | £109 |
| Metro 300 | Full service, including battery health test and firmware check | 2,500 km or 12 months | £89 |
| Cargo 700 | Full service, including battery health test and firmware check | 1,500 km or 6 months | £139 |

A service includes tightening all bolts, checking brake pads and rotors, adjusting gears, checking tyre condition, a
battery health test, a firmware check and a ride test. Parts used are charged in addition. The first check (after 100 km
or 4 weeks) is free at any Fernhill dealer.

## Warranty repairs

Repairs that are covered by the warranty are free, including labour and the replacement part. Wear parts and accidental
damage are not covered. If we inspect a bike and find the fault is not covered, we tell you the price before we do any
work, and an inspection of one hour or less is free.

## How long a repair takes

A standard service takes 3 working days at a service centre. A repair needing a part can take longer; we normally have
batteries, chargers, brake pads, tyres, chains and displays in stock.

## Booking

Book at fernhillbikes.example/service, by calling 01632 960 410, or at any authorised dealer that offers service. Bring
the bike, its charger and your order number. Dealers that can do motor and controller work are marked as Master Service
in the store locations document.
