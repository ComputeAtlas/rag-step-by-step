# Fernhill Cargo 700: Owner's Manual

Edition 4, December 2025. Covers Cargo 700 bikes with serial numbers starting `FH-C7-`. Firmware described: v2.5.
Keep this manual with your bike's receipt. Businesses running several Cargo 700 bikes should also read the Cargo 700
business plan document.

The Cargo 700 is Fernhill's long-tail cargo e-bike. It carries a rider, shopping, tools or two children, and it
keeps the motor in a calm, steady zone while it does so. The number in the name is the battery size in watt-hours,
rounded down to the nearest hundred (the real figure is 720 Wh).

## 2. Before you load it

1. Stand the bike on its kickstand on level ground. Switch it off before loading or unloading.
2. Never go above the 200 kg total load. This includes you, any passengers, bags and the bike's accessories. The rack
   alone is rated for 80 kg.
3. Spread weight low and centred over the rear axle. Heavy items go at the bottom of the rear bags, nearest to the saddle.
4. Strap loads down. A shifting load changes how the bike steers.
5. Child seats must be fitted by a Fernhill dealer or by following the dealer fitting sheet. Children must wear helmets.
6. Allow more stopping distance: a fully loaded Cargo 700 needs about 40% longer to stop.

Register your bike at fernhillbikes.example/register within 30 days of purchase. You will need the order number and
the serial number, which is stamped under the frame, next to the pedals, and starts with `FH-C7-`. See the warranty policy for what
registration gives you for the year you bought the bike, and the business plan document if the bike is part of a fleet.

## 3. The display and controls

The Dash 2 display has a colour screen, a remote pad (plus, minus, mode) and a walk-assist button.

- **Plus / minus:** change assist level.
- **Mode (short press):** trip distance, total distance, range left, load setting.
- **Mode (hold 2 seconds):** opens the menu: Battery, Display, Bike (service reminder, firmware version), Load.
- **Walk assist (hold):** up to 4 km/h. This is slower than on the other Fernhill models because of the bike's weight.
- **Auto-off:** the display switches itself off after 10 minutes without use.

Haul is the strongest assist level. It gives the motor 250% of your pedal effort and is meant for starting off with a
heavy load or climbing short hills. It empties the battery faster than the other levels.

From firmware v2.5 the Load menu lets you set Empty, Half or Full so the range estimate counts the weight you carry.
Before v2.5 the estimate ignored the load (see the firmware release notes).

## 4. Charging and battery care

Use the charger supplied with your bike, FH-2320-A. It is a 48 V charger, and it is the only charger that fits and
suits the Cargo 700 battery. Do not use the 36 V charger from a Ridge 500 or Metro 300.

1. Switch the bike off.
2. Plug the charger into the wall, then into the charging port on the down tube.
3. The charger light is red while charging and green when full.
4. The battery is heavy (4.6 kg). Take care when lifting it out after unlocking.

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Daily use | Charge when convenient. Partial charges are fine. |
| Not riding for more than 2 weeks | Store at 40 to 60% charge, indoors, at 10 to 20 °C |
| Storage for months | Check every 3 months and top up to 50 to 60% |
| Business fleets using the bike daily | Charge each evening; Battery care mode (80% limit) is a good choice for short routes |
| Cold weather | Keep the battery indoors until you ride. Range falls by roughly a quarter near 0 °C |
| Leaving the battery empty | Never (error E09) |

Battery care mode (80% charge limit) was added in firmware v2.5; switch it on under Menu > Battery.

## 6. Display error codes

When the controller detects a fault the display shows E and two digits. Switch the bike off, wait ten seconds and
switch it on again. If the code returns, use the table. These codes are specific to the Cargo 700. Other Fernhill
models use some of the same numbers for different faults.

| Code | Meaning on the Cargo 700 | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Battery not detected: no communication with the battery pack | Remove and refit the battery until it clicks. Wipe the contacts with a dry cloth. If it returns, see a dealer. |
| E02 | Motor not responding | Check the motor cable plug at the bottom bracket is pushed fully home. |
| E03 | Torque sensor signal out of range | Switch off. Restart with the bike upright on its kickstand and no weight on the pedals. |
| E04 | Speed sensor: no signal | Check the magnet on the rear wheel. The gap to the sensor must be 3 to 5 mm. |
| E05 | Walk-assist button pressed at switch-on | Release the button, restart. |
| E06 | Battery over-temperature (above 60 °C) | Stop and park in shade for 30 minutes. |
| E07 | Battery too cold (below -10 °C) | Warm the battery indoors for an hour. |
| E08 | Motor over-temperature (above 85 °C) | Stop and wait 30 minutes. Use a lower assist level on long climbs with a load. |
| E09 | Battery voltage too low | Connect the charger. A deeply discharged pack can take 30 minutes to start charging. |
| E10 | Charger fault: charger voltage outside range | Use the 48 V FH-2320-A charger supplied with the bike. A 36 V charger will trigger this. |
| E11 | Brake cut-off switch active at switch-on (either lever) | Release both brake levers and restart. |
| E12 | Shift-assist sensor fault | Switch off and on. If it returns, have the sensor checked. The bike rides normally. |
| E13 | Light circuit short or overload (includes the brake light) | Check the light wiring and plugs. Switch the lights off. |
| E14 | Display and controller firmware do not match | Update both to the same version in the Fernhill Service app or at a dealer. |
| E15 | Controller over-current (often a stall on a slope with a heavy load) | Switch off, wait 10 seconds, restart. Reduce the load or walk the bike up the slope. |
| E16 | Battery cells out of balance | Leave the battery on the charger for 12 hours. |
| E17 | Battery calibration drift (added in firmware v2.4): range estimate unreliable | Ride the battery down below 10%, then charge to 100% without interruption. |
| E18 | Battery not locked fully | Turn the key to lock the battery, then restart. |
| E19 | Motor phase fault | Do not ride. Contact a dealer. |
| E20 | Display memory fault | The display needs replacing (part FH-5102-A). |

## 7. Firmware

Update with the Fernhill Service app (free for phones). The update takes about 12 minutes and needs the battery above
50%. The Cargo 700 shipped with firmware v2.2. Display and controller update together and must carry the same version
(otherwise E14 appears). Your version is shown under Menu > Bike. The Cargo 700 battery (FH-2292-A) works with every
firmware version.

## 9. Safety

- Wear a helmet. Children must wear helmets too.
- Do not go over 200 kg total load, or 80 kg on the rack.
- Do not leave children on the bike when it is unattended, even on the kickstand.
- Do not tune the motor or controller. It voids the warranty and makes the bike illegal on public roads.
- Assistance ends at 25 km/h by law.
- Brake early. A heavy bike takes longer to stop, especially downhill.
- Do not ride through water deeper than the bottom of the motor.

## 10. Carrying children and cargo well

- Seat the heaviest passenger directly over the rear axle, behind the rider.
- Small children should ride only in a fitted child seat, with the harness done up.
- Check the kickstand is fully down before anybody climbs on or off.
- A load of 100 kg on the deck adds about 2 seconds to a standing start on a gentle slope; use Haul to start, then
  change to Tour once you are moving.
- Cover loads in rain, and remember that wet brakes need longer.
- On a downhill, use both brakes together, and stay in a low gear so that the motor is not asked to do anything
  surprising.

## 11. Problems without an error code

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to try |
|---|---|---|
| Range estimate far too high with a heavy load (firmware before v2.5) | The estimate ignores the load | Update to v2.5 and set the Load menu. |
| Bike pulls to one side | Load unevenly spread | Rebalance bags and check the tyre pressure. |
| Brakes fade on a long hill | Hot pads | Stop, let them cool. Check the pad thickness. |
| Rack rattles | Loose rack bolts | Tighten rack bolts to 9 Nm. |
| Kickstand sags | Loose kickstand bolts | Tighten to 25 Nm and check for cracks. |

## 12. Regulatory information

- The Cargo 700 is an electrically power-assisted cycle. Its FH-M95 mid-drive motor has a nominal continuous rated power of 250 W and
  stops giving assistance at 25 km/h, or sooner if you stop pedalling. This means that it is treated as a normal bicycle in
  the UK, Ireland, the Netherlands and Germany.
- The bike is designed to meet the European standard for electrically power-assisted cycles, EN 15194. The declaration of
  conformity is available at fernhillbikes.example/doc.
- The sound pressure level of the motor at the rider's ear is below 70 dB(A).
- The vibration at the handlebars and saddle is below the level that requires a warning.
- The serial number (starting `FH-C7-`) is stamped under the frame, next to the pedals. Keep a note of it.
- The manufacturer is Fernhill Bikes Ltd, Hollinwick.

## 13. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Assist level | How much extra power the motor adds to your pedalling |
| Cadence | How fast you turn the pedals, in revolutions per minute |
| Charge cycle | The use of energy equal to the full battery's capacity in total |
| Controller | The electronic unit that decides how much power the motor gives |
| Display | The screen and buttons on the handlebars (the Dash 2 on this bike) |
| Firmware | The software inside the display and the controller |
| Nm (newton metres) | A measure of twisting force. This bike's motor peaks at 95 Nm |
| Sensor | On this bike a torque sensor in the motor reads how hard you push the pedals |
| Walk assist | Gentle motor help while you push the bike beside you |
| Wh (watt-hours) | The energy stored in the battery |

A short note on the motor type: a mid-drive motor uses the bike's gears, so a low gear gives the most climbing power with a heavy load.

## 13. Questions owners ask about the Cargo 700

**How much can I carry?** 200 kg in total, including you and any passengers. The rack alone is rated for 80 kg. Up to two child seats can be fitted, each for a child of up to 22 kg.

**How far will I really go?** Unloaded in Eco, up to 100 km. Fully loaded in Eco, up to 70 km. In Haul, 40 km unloaded and 30 km loaded.

**Why is walk assist slower than on my friend's Ridge 500?** The Cargo 700 weighs 41.5 kg, so its walk assist is limited to 4 km/h for your safety; the other models allow 6 km/h.

**Which charger do I need?** FH-2320-A, a 48 V 4 A charger. The Ridge 500 and Metro 300 chargers are 36 V and will show E10 on the Cargo 700.

**Which brake pads do I need?** FH-3105-B (sintered). The organic FH-3105-A pad from the Ridge 500 is not suitable for the Cargo 700.

**How often must I service it?** Every 1,500 km or 6 months, whichever comes first. A Cargo 700 works harder than the other models, so its interval is shorter.

**What is the Load menu?** Added in firmware v2.5. Set Empty, Half or Full so the range estimate counts your cargo.

**Can I use it for deliveries?** Only if it was bought on a Fernhill business plan. See the warranty section and the business plan document.

## 14. How the motor assistance works

The Cargo 700 has no throttle: it helps only when you pedal. The FH-M95 motor has a torque sensor that measures how hard you push the cranks, and the
controller multiplies that force by the assist level you chose: 40% in Eco, 80% in Tour, 140% in Sport and 250% in Haul. With a heavy load Haul is
often the right level for starting and for short climbs. The motor is rated at 250 W nominal and its peak torque is 95 Nm, the highest of the three models.

Because it carries more weight, the Cargo 700 uses a 48 V battery rather than 36 V. A higher voltage lets the same power flow at a lower current, which keeps the wiring and
the motor cooler. This is why the charger is 48 V as well (FH-2320-A), and why a 36 V charger from the other models shows E10.

The controller limits the help in four ways: at 25 km/h by law; below 10% battery to save the charge; at 85 °C motor temperature (E08, with a 30-minute rest); and in
the first second after a standing start with a heavy load, when firmware v2.3 and later is gentler on the torque. If the motor stalls on a slope with a heavy load,
the over-current protection acts and the display shows E15.

From firmware v2.5 you can tell the bike how much you are carrying (Menu > Load). The range estimate then takes the weight into account. Before that, the estimate
assumed an empty bike, which is why heavily loaded riders saw ranges that were up to 25% too optimistic.
