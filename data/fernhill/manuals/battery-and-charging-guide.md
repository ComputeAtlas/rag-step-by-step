# Fernhill Battery and Charging Guide

Edition January 2026. A plain-English guide to the batteries on the Ridge 500, Metro 300 and Cargo 700, with a few numbers
that help you plan your rides.

## Reading the numbers

- **Voltage (V):** the "pressure" of the battery. Ridge 500 and Metro 300 packs are 36 V; the Cargo 700 pack is 48 V.
- **Amp-hours (Ah):** how much charge it holds. Ridge 500: 14 Ah. Metro 300: 9 Ah. Cargo 700: 15 Ah.
- **Watt-hours (Wh):** volts times amp-hours. This is the energy in the battery and the figure to compare. Ridge 500: 36 x 14 = 504 Wh.
  Metro 300: 36 x 9 = 324 Wh. Cargo 700: 48 x 15 = 720 Wh. The model name is this number rounded down to the nearest hundred.

## How much energy the bike uses

Divide the battery energy by the claimed range to get the consumption in watt-hours per kilometre. These are the figures behind the
published ranges (flat road, 75 kg rider, 20 °C).

| Model | Assist level | Range | Energy per km |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ridge 500 | Eco | 110 km | 4.6 Wh |
| Ridge 500 | Tour | 80 km | 6.3 Wh |
| Ridge 500 | Sport | 60 km | 8.4 Wh |
| Ridge 500 | Boost | 45 km | 11.2 Wh |
| Metro 300 | Eco | 80 km | 4.1 Wh |
| Metro 300 | Tour | 60 km | 5.4 Wh |
| Metro 300 | Sport | 45 km | 7.2 Wh |
| Cargo 700 | Eco (unloaded) | 100 km | 7.2 Wh |
| Cargo 700 | Tour (unloaded) | 75 km | 9.6 Wh |
| Cargo 700 | Sport (unloaded) | 55 km | 13.1 Wh |
| Cargo 700 | Haul (unloaded) | 40 km | 18.0 Wh |

Charging a full battery from empty costs a few pence of electricity: 0.5 kWh for a Ridge 500, 0.32 kWh for a Metro 300 and 0.72 kWh
for a Cargo 700 (slightly more, because of charging losses).

## What a charge cycle is

A full charge cycle is the use of an amount of energy equal to the full battery. Use half the battery, charge it, use half
again: that is one cycle, not two. The count is in Menu > Battery > Cycles. Firmware v2.2 counted every plug-in as a cycle by mistake; v2.3
corrected this. A battery loses capacity slowly with every cycle. The warranty terms are written around cycles for this
reason: under the 2026 policy 800 cycles, under the 2025 policy 500 cycles.

## Charging, step by step

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Bulk | The charger delivers its full current, fast, up to about 80% |
| Top-off | The current falls so the cells are not stressed, up to 100% |
| Finish | The charger light turns green and the current stops |

That is why charging to 80% takes much less time than charging to 100%: 3 h 30 min against 5 h on the Ridge 500, 2 h 30 min
against 3 h 30 min on the Metro 300, and 3 h against 4 h 30 min on the Cargo 700.

## Making the battery last

1. Avoid storing it full or empty for long periods; 40 to 60% is best.
2. Do not charge when it is hot from riding; wait 20 minutes.
3. Do not leave it in a hot car or in direct sun.
4. Use Battery care mode (firmware v2.5 or later) if your rides are short.
5. Use the right charger. The Ridge 500 and Metro 300 use FH-2310-B and the Cargo 700 uses FH-2320-A.
6. Once a year, ask a dealer for a battery health test (about 15 minutes, free with a service).

## Signs the battery is wearing out

- The range at the same assist level, route and temperature is much shorter than it used to be.
- The health figure under Menu > Battery has fallen below 70%.
- The display shows E16 (cells out of balance, Ridge 500 and Cargo 700) often.

Whether a worn battery is covered depends on your bike's purchase year; see the warranty policies. A new pack costs £449
(Ridge 500, FH-2291-B), £329 (Metro 300, FH-2219-B) or £599 (Cargo 700, FH-2292-A).
