# New Owner's Riding Tips

Edition January 2026. Practical advice for your first weeks on a Fernhill e-bike, whatever the model.

## Start gently

An electric bike feels different from a normal one the first time. The motor reacts to how hard you push the pedals, so a firm
push from a standstill gives you a quick start. For the first ride:

1. Pick a quiet, flat place away from traffic.
2. Start in Eco. Pedal gently and feel the motor join in.
3. Try a few starts and stops. Cover the brakes with your fingers until you are used to the extra speed.
4. Move up through Tour and Sport only when you feel at ease. Boost (Ridge 500) and Haul (Cargo 700) are strong; use them on
   hills or with a load, not on a crowded path.

## Gears and the motor

- Change down before you stop, so you can set off in a low gear. Starting in a high gear strains the motor and the chain.
- On the Ridge 500 and Cargo 700 the motor cuts out briefly while you change gear. Ease off the pedals for a smoother shift.
- On the Metro 300 you can change hub gears while stationary, which is handy at traffic lights.

## Braking

- Use both brakes together. The front brake does most of the stopping, the rear keeps the bike steady.
- E-bikes are heavier than normal bikes and need more stopping distance. Leave extra space, especially in the wet.
- A new bike's pads need bedding in: do ten firm stops from walking pace.

## Getting the most from the battery

- Charge it when it suits you; partial charges are fine.
- Use a lower assist level on flat ground. Range is best in Eco.
- Keep your tyres at the right pressure: it changes the range more than most people expect.
- Avoid very cold storage. Bring the battery indoors on winter nights.

## Your first service

The first free check is at 100 km or 4 weeks, whichever comes first. A dealer tightens bolts, checks the spokes and brakes. After that
a full service is due every 2,000 km or 12 months (Ridge 500), 2,500 km or 12 months (Metro 300) or 1,500 km or 6 months (Cargo 700).

## Registering and updating

- Register your bike at fernhillbikes.example/register within 30 days. What registration gives you depends on the year you bought
  the bike.
- Install the Fernhill Service app and keep the firmware up to date. A new version can fix bugs and add features.
- Read the safety notices. If a charger or part is recalled, we will write to you if you are registered.

## Locking and security

- Use a good lock and lock the frame and a wheel to something fixed.
- Remove the battery if you leave the bike for a long time; it is the most valuable part.
- Note your serial number and keep your receipt in case of theft or a warranty claim.

## Riding in traffic

- Ride where drivers can see you, about a metre from the kerb, not in the gutter. Make eye contact at junctions.
- The motor makes you faster than other cyclists, so a driver may judge your speed wrongly. Slow down slightly at junctions and when passing parked cars.
- Use hand signals early, and use the lights after dark. The Metro 300 and Cargo 700 have lights powered by the main battery; a Ridge 500
  rider can fit an accessory light of up to 6 W to the accessory port.
- Do not ride on the pavement unless the law where you live allows it. Rules for e-bikes on shared paths differ by country.
- In the rain, brake earlier and more gently. Disc brakes work well in the wet, but the road does not.
- If you carry a child on the Cargo 700, start and stop smoothly and avoid riding on busy roads at the start.

## Parking

Lock the bike every time you leave it, even for a minute. If you can, park it under cover. Take the battery with you if the bike will be outside for
hours, and put a note of your serial number in your phone.
