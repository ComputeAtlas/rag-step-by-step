# Dealer Service Checklist (Full Service)

Edition January 2026. For Service and Master Service dealers carrying out the full service of a Ridge 500, Metro 300 or
Cargo 700. Customers can read it to see what a service includes (see service and repair pricing for prices).

## Before you start

1. Ask the rider about problems, error codes and unusual noises.
2. Record the serial number, the distance on the display and the date of the last service.
3. Connect the Fernhill Service app and read the last 20 error codes.
4. Check the battery health and the cycle count. Make a note if health is below 80%.

## Checklist

| Step | Ridge 500 | Metro 300 | Cargo 700 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service interval | 2,000 km or 12 months | 2,500 km or 12 months | 1,500 km or 6 months |
| Brake pad replacement thickness | 1.5 mm | 1.5 mm | 1.5 mm |
| Brake pad part | FH-3105-A | FH-3110-A | FH-3105-B |
| Rotor minimum thickness | 1.8 mm | 1.8 mm | 1.8 mm |
| Chain replacement at | 0.5% stretch | 0.75% stretch | 0.5% stretch |
| Chain part | FH-7101-A | FH-7102-A | FH-7103-A |
| Crank arm bolts | 50 Nm | 40 Nm | 55 Nm |
| Rear axle | 12 Nm (thru-axle) | 40 Nm (nuts) | 55 Nm (nuts) |
| Rotor bolts | 4 Nm | 4 Nm | 4 Nm |
| Brake fluid replacement | Every 2 years | Every 2 years | Every 18 months |
| Tyre pressure after service | 1.4 to 2.5 bar | 3.0 to 4.0 bar | 2.5 to 3.2 bar |

## Steps in order

1. **Wash and inspect.** Wipe the bike with a damp cloth. Look for cracks around welds, the head tube, the rear dropouts and the rack
   mounts.
2. **Wheels.** Check spoke tension and that the wheels run true. True a wheel if needed (£18).
3. **Tyres.** Check wear and cuts. Set the pressure.
4. **Brakes.** Measure pad thickness, check rotors, check lever feel. Bleed the system if the lever is spongy (£35 per brake).
5. **Drivetrain.** Measure chain stretch, clean and lubricate. On the Metro 300, adjust the hub gear cable.
6. **Bolts.** Check all bolts against the torque table in the owner's manual. On the Cargo 700 also check the rack bolts (9 Nm) and the
   kickstand bolts (25 Nm).
7. **Electrics.** Check connectors at the motor, display and battery for corrosion. Clean the battery contacts. Test lights.
8. **Battery.** Run the battery health test (15 minutes, included in the service). Record health, cycles and any warnings.
9. **Firmware.** Update display and controller to the latest version, making sure the builds match.
10. **Reset the service reminder** under Menu > Bike (firmware v2.6 or later).
11. **Ride test.** Test each assist level, the brakes and the walk assist. Look for error codes.
12. **Paperwork.** Stamp the service record in the manual. Give the rider a note of anything that will need attention soon.

## Notes for the Cargo 700

- Check the double kickstand returns and holds the loaded bike.
- Check child seat fittings if fitted.
- The service reminder on firmware v2.6 counts 2,000 km instead of the real 1,500 km interval. Do not rely on it; tell the rider the correct
  date.

## Time allowance

A full service takes a technician about 2 hours (3 hours for the Cargo 700). Customers pay the flat service prices in the service and repair pricing document, and the bike is normally returned within 3 working days.
