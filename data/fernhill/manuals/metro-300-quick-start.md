# Fernhill Metro 300: Quick-Start Guide

Edition 2, January 2026. For the full details read the Metro 300 owner's manual.

The box is 1,500 mm long, 300 mm wide and 800 mm tall. The packed bike weighs 24 kg. The battery arrives fitted at
about 50% charge.

## Set-up in eight steps (about 20 minutes)

1. Cut the straps and lift the bike out. Keep the box for 30 days in case you need to return the bike.
2. Turn the handlebars to face forward and tighten the stem bolts to 6 Nm, alternating between them.
3. Fit the right pedal (marked R) clockwise and the left pedal (marked L) anti-clockwise. Tighten both to 35 Nm.
4. Set the saddle so your leg is almost straight at the lowest pedal position.
5. Pump both tyres to 3.0 to 4.0 bar.
6. Check both brakes and the mudguard stays.
7. Charge the battery fully (about 3 hours 30 minutes from 0 to 100%).
8. Switch on with the remote pad. Hold the plus button for two seconds to test the lights.

## Your first week

- Try all three assist levels on a quiet road.
- After the first 100 km or 4 weeks, whichever comes first, have the spokes and bolts checked. The hub gear cable is
  adjusted at the first service.
- Register your bike at fernhillbikes.example/register within 30 days of purchase.
- Note your serial number (`FH-M3-` followed by the year and a number).
