# Fernhill Cargo 700: Quick-Start Guide

Edition 2, January 2026. For the full details read the Cargo 700 owner's manual.

## What is in the box

| Item | Quantity |

| Charger FH-2320-A, 48 V 4 A | 1 |

| Quick-start guide and printed owner's manual | 1 each |

The Cargo 700 arrives on a pallet. The crate measures 2,100 mm x 450 mm x 1,000 mm and the packed bike weighs 49 kg.
Two people are needed to lift it. The battery arrives fitted at about 50% charge.

## Set-up in ten steps (about 45 minutes)

1. Check the crate for damage before the courier leaves and write any damage on the delivery note.
2. Cut the straps and lift the bike out with a second person.
3. Turn the handlebars to face forward and tighten the stem bolts to 6 Nm.
4. Fit the right pedal (marked R) clockwise and the left pedal (marked L) anti-clockwise. Tighten both to 35 Nm.
5. Lower the double kickstand and stand the bike on level ground.
6. Pump both tyres to 2.5 bar (3.2 bar when loaded).
7. Check the rack bolts are tight (9 Nm).
8. Squeeze each brake lever; they must feel firm.
9. Charge the battery fully with the 48 V charger (about 4 hours 30 minutes from 0 to 100%).
10. Switch on with the display and try the walk assist, which moves the bike at up to 4 km/h.

## Your first week

- Do not exceed 200 kg total load. Carry heavy things low and spread them evenly.
- After the first 100 km or 4 weeks, whichever comes first, have the bolts and spokes checked.
- The Cargo 700 needs a full service every 1,500 km or 6 months, whichever comes first.
- Register your bike at fernhillbikes.example/register within 30 days of purchase.
- Note your serial number (`FH-C7-` followed by the year and a number).
