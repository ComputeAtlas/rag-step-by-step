# Fernhill Ridge 500: Quick-Start Guide

Edition 2, January 2026. For the full details read the Ridge 500 owner's manual.

| Item | Quantity |

The box is 1,450 mm long, 330 mm wide and 800 mm tall. The packed bike weighs 29 kg. The battery is fitted but
switched off at the factory; it arrives at about 50% charge.

## Set-up in ten steps (about 30 minutes)

1. Cut the straps and take the bike out. Keep the box for 30 days in case you need to return the bike.
2. Remove the protective foam from the fork and frame.
3. Turn the handlebars to face forward and tighten the stem bolts to 5 Nm, alternating between them.
4. Check the display angle and tighten its clamp by hand.
5. Fit the right pedal (marked R) by turning it clockwise. The left pedal (marked L) turns anti-clockwise. Tighten both to 35 Nm.
6. Set the saddle height. Your leg should be almost straight with the pedal at its lowest point.
7. Pump both tyres to 1.4 to 2.0 bar for trails or 2.2 to 2.5 bar for roads.
8. Squeeze each brake lever. They must feel firm and stop before they touch the handlebar.
9. Charge the battery fully (about 5 hours from 0 to 100%).
10. Switch the bike on with the button on the display and test the walk assist.

## Your first week

- Ride in Eco or Tour to get a feeling for how the motor helps.
- After the first 100 km or 4 weeks, whichever comes first, check all bolts and spokes yourself or book a free check at
  a Fernhill dealer.
- Register your bike at fernhillbikes.example/register within 30 days of purchase.
- Note your serial number (`FH-R5-` followed by the year and a number) and keep your order number safe.
