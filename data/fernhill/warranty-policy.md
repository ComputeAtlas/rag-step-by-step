# Fernhill Bikes: Warranty Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. This policy applies to every Fernhill bike bought new on or after that date.

## What is covered

| Part | How long |
|---|---|
| Frame | Lifetime, for the original owner |
| Motor and controller | 2 years |
| Battery | 3 years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first |
| Display, lights and wiring | 2 years |
| Paint and finish | 1 year |

All periods start on the date of purchase.

A battery counts as defective if it holds less than 70% of its original capacity while it is still under warranty.

**Register your bike within 30 days of purchase and your battery warranty grows to 4 years.** The 800-cycle limit
stays the same.

## What is not covered

- Parts that wear out with normal riding: tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains, cassettes and grips.
- Damage from crashes, misuse, or riding through water deeper than the bottom of the motor.
- Batteries charged with any charger other than the Fernhill charger supplied with the bike.
- Bikes used for rental or delivery work, unless they are a Cargo 700 bought on a Fernhill business plan.

## How to make a claim

1. Find your bike's serial number. It is stamped under the frame, next to the pedals, and starts with `FH-`.
2. Write to warranty@fernhillbikes.example with your order number, the serial number and a photo of the problem.
3. We reply within 2 working days. If we approve the claim, we repair or replace the part within 10 working days.

If a part is replaced under warranty, the replacement is covered for the rest of the original warranty period.
